Official source: pytest 9.1.1 tag, doc/en/changelog.rst.

Breaking behavior in pytest 9.1.0:
- requires --doctest-modules;
- applies to inline autouse fixtures with module, package, or session scope;
- those fixtures may execute twice because modules can be collected as pytest.Module and DoctestModule.

Deprecation surfaces material to target mapping:
- class-scoped fixtures defined as instance methods without @classmethod;
- request.getfixturevalue() during teardown for a fixture not already requested;
- non-Collection iterables as parametrize argvalues;
- private config.inicfg;
- FixtureDef baseid/nodeid registration APIs;
- hook configuration using pytest markers;
- --pastebin;
- private FixtureDef.has_location;
- pytest.console_main;
- yield_fixture.
