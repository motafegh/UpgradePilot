## Disposition

All 27 focused artifact and investigation tests passed against the installed wheel. The additional real case gives a second publication-positive, target-unresolved result. The bounded screen did not qualify an exact target case. Retain the applicability evaluator and defer a target-tag acquisition implementation. Resume when a current candidate has an admitted exact update and an available full descriptor from its relevant installer, ideally a target-owned structured artifact with binding to the revision, attempt, job and install step. A wheel match then answers one environment's published wheel availability. Source builds, target behavior, and broader maintainer action continue to require their own evidence.
