# First-view comparison protocol for readiness review

This is an operator document. Provide only [the reviewer task](REVIEWER_TASK.md) and each reviewer's assigned local case files during an authorized study. The answer key and allocation remain separate. The [report plan](../../../plans/MAINTAINER_REPORT_PRESERVATION_AND_USEFULNESS_EVALUATION_PLAN.md#first-view-comparison-on-saved-reports) owns the evaluation boundaries.

## Question and fixed conditions

Can a maintainer recover the correct finding, supporting evidence and limits more readily when the actual **Review at a glance** opening is present? The comparison concerns the opening over a fixed report. It does not compare UpgradePilot with unrestricted ordinary review. Each case's two conditions use the same decoded report, detailed rendering, original-text sources and lack of original text for other source references. The baseline is renderer 3 with just the first-view block and its detail heading removed. Both conditions show the same renderer and generator metadata. The comparison tool is offline preparation code.

The [prior packet](../2026-11-08-maintainer-report-first-view/REVIEW_PACKET.md) names the exact captures and distinct evidence horizons. HTTPX is the saved caller/signature report, OpenCV the saved normal artifact report, and Soup Sieve the earlier partial report where the tag lookup blocked the support-drop branch. Do not substitute a later capture. These cases are exposed to the preparer. No prior exposure of an actual reviewer is assumed or established.

## Allocation and session

Use two independent reviewers with relevant Python dependency-review experience and an independent review of the expected findings before activation. Confirm no involvement in authoring these reports, the code or task labels, and no prior access to the expected answers or the cases' prepared outputs. Exclude affected assignments before collection and record the exact reason. Record reviewer experience and adjudication responsibilities without publishing personal identities. Eligibility and availability are currently unconfirmed.

| Order | Reviewer slot 1 | Reviewer slot 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | HTTPX, full report | Soup Sieve, first view plus full report |
| 2 | OpenCV, first view plus full report | OpenCV, full report |
| 3 | Soup Sieve, full report | HTTPX, first view plus full report |

Each reviewer sees each case once. The conditions alternate per reviewer, with the two-to-one imbalance reversed. HTTPX and Soup Sieve swap positions and OpenCV stays in the middle. Case, position and reviewer effects remain possible at this size. Give equivalent local text navigation and original-source access, identical instructions, and the same neutral setup help. Preflight the reviewer-only package and permissions before starting. Keep the other condition, answer key, historical review and tools outside their session. No fresh evidence fetches. Use one ten-minute ceiling per case, record partial answers and stop at the ceiling. Record elapsed time from first report access to submission, the actual source opens, any clarification, and whether help was substantive. Count a wrong-source attempt as an open attempt and preserve the error. Stop before debriefing until all answers for that reviewer are captured.

Record each of the five answers separately as `correct`, `partial`, `incorrect`, `missing` or `not_scored`, with the reason. Retain the raw response, references and navigation observations. Grade before revealing allocation to the adjudicator where feasible and disclose remaining exposure. Critical failures include a material unsupported finding, wrong identity, erased condition or invented action. Timeouts, substantive coaching and inaccessible material remain separate operational outcomes. A case may count as recovered only if exact identity, finding or stop reason, proof scope and material uncertainty are correct, with a valid supporting reference and a bounded response to the observation question.

For each case compare outcome and burden descriptively. If the first view preserves required meaning in all three cases and produces concrete unaided task recovery or navigation benefit over the full view on at least two contrasting cases, consider retaining the opening and planning broader evaluation. A critical first-view-induced error calls for repair and re-evaluation. Comparable correct results with no concrete difference favor retaining the simpler interpretation of the evidence and deferring further presentation work. Missing reviewers, unapproved source delivery, setup inequality or inconclusive observations leave usefulness unestablished. These six observations cannot estimate population or timing effects.
