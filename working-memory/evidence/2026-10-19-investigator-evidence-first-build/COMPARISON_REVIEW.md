## Decision and remaining responsibility

The planned all-four-adequate gate is not met. This completed negative comparison does not justify a second prompt, a worksheet, a new model, protected-case exposure, autonomous-discovery engineering or product adoption automatically. Complete delivery, valid typed citations and more visible assertions have not established reliable source interpretation in these samples. Adding fixtures alone would measure more cases without repairing the demonstrated relation/attribution/authority failures.

My recommendation is to make the next bounded decision about a **general deterministic source-fact/relation baseline**, before another interpretation-tuning increment. That design should establish what explicit signatures and forwarded arguments can be extracted, how complete source/capture identity is retained, and when dynamic binding/aliases/partial context must stay unresolved. It must not encode a special rule for this exposed keyword or publish model recommendations as accepted facts. It is a proposed next design responsibility, not implemented behavior.

The full product still needs repository-specific effect interpretation, applicability/runtime evaluation, evidence validation and useful maintainer decisions. A static baseline would cover only an admitted part of that path. Existing native CI/runtime evaluators and deterministic action synthesis retain their authority. Qualification on new protected material, independent evaluation-to-synthesis boundaries and maintainer usefulness remain separate decisions after a method earns development acceptance. A different deployment/method remains a credible alternative requiring its own finite question, proof and budget.
