## Candidate milestone — proposed, not accepted

For a declared supported subset of public Python Dependabot updates, UpgradePilot helps a maintainer understand and verify a material relationship between the update and their project, or a precisely located missing premise, through an inspectable saved report. Success requires a meaningful gain over the current report/source baseline on an important target-relevance task; reformatting source text or returning generic unknowns is insufficient.

This is a candidate delivery goal. It does not claim that every PR has a known impact, that all supported cases get a positive diagnosis, or that broad compatibility/action permission is established. Materially useful refutations and justified unresolved outcomes belong in the proof alongside positive/conditional findings. The supported envelope, cases and exact acceptance threshold are still to be settled.

For a product delivery milestone, the result must eventually be reachable from the declared ordinary PR input through the admitted user-facing flow. An isolated curated experiment can qualify a method or expose a prerequisite, but cannot itself complete that delivery claim. The implementation/integration route is not selected by stating this goal; its feasibility is one of the forthcoming scope and migration decisions.

Illustration from HTTPX: the current normal report can expose copied app/proxies-removal and cert-deprecation context, but it does not normally produce the relevant target/framework call relationship. A richer historical packet supports a conditional older/newer caller/signature contrast, with actual installed framework version unresolved. A future milestone must earn the relation through the selected normal producer path rather than copy the historical curated diagnosis into its inputs. This example motivates the outcome; it does not select HTTPX as the whole product or prove that the proposed structural plan alone can deliver it.
