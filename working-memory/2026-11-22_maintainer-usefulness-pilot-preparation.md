## Draft operation and decision rule

The owner-private operator sheet proposes two slots with opposite conditions for every case and opposite starting conditions. Each case appears once per reviewer. Log task answers, source-navigation path, elapsed time excluding setup, assistance, and technical failures before debriefing. The owner-private draft key records source locators, material findings and unknowns, critical false conclusions, and admissible alternate next checks. Start scoring at evidence faithfulness and comparability. Do not equate time, preference, or agreement with task recovery. Use the existing plan's usefulness gate of practical assistance on more than one contrasting responsibility without critical errors or author coaching. Retain no-benefit, incomplete, and incomparable results.

If the report helps on matched evidence, the next investment can target the demonstrated assistance. If the facts are present but hard to find, test a narrower presentation change. If a necessary premise is absent from both arms, route the missing evidence to its owner. No result from this packet design can establish performance against unrestricted browsing or broader target coverage.
