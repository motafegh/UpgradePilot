The source trace does **not** reveal a missing universal evidence type or a need to replace current domain owners. It reveals a cross-domain product gap:

- one evolving investigation identity/current-state composition owner across native records, proposals, evaluated propositions/candidates, missing premises and relevant history;

- an interface by which synthesis consumes evaluated knowledge while retaining separate recommendation/action authority.

**Adapter rule carried into B2:** add an adapter only when a cross-domain consumer genuinely needs a stable reference/projection that a native owner does not already provide. An adapter must preserve the native record's identity, scope, provenance and limitations; it must not reinterpret or duplicate the domain fact merely for workspace uniformity.

**B1 conclusion:** the existing product is not missing a generic “reasoning core.” Its strongest current property is the separation of domain-owned evidence/evaluation boundaries. B2 should therefore compare composition approaches by how well they preserve those owners while adding evolving cross-domain state. ### Accepted structural direction

Ali explicitly accepted **Alternative B: a distinct composed/evolving Investigation Workspace**, after challenging the originally over-cautious migration proposal. The accepted constraints carried into B3 are:

1. **Composition, not reinterpretation.** Native provider/domain records and evaluator results remain authoritative. The Workspace retains or references them with identity, scope, provenance and limitations; it does not manufacture generic replacements merely for uniformity.
2. **Lifecycle owner, not truth owner.** The Workspace owns investigation identity/revision, cross-domain relationships, proposal/request/observation/evaluation lineage, missing-premise state, bounded views and history/recovery semantics. Domain evaluators still decide domain propositions; maintainer synthesis still owns action-relative output.
3. **Canonical target, not a permanent parallel experiment.** The Workspace is intended to become the product's canonical investigation state/contract. `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` is existing architecture to migrate from, not a second long-term product path that must survive beside the Workspace.
4. **Replacement-oriented migration.** Migration safety is used to preserve proof and reversibility while moving to the accepted target. A temporary snapshot adapter/projection may exist only when a concrete caller/proof/compatibility need justifies it, and its removal/reassessment condition must be explicit. Current consumers and passing tests establish migration pressure; they do not independently justify permanent retention.
5. **No stack of “minimal” architectures.** Design the coherent target responsibility first. Later implementation may be split into evidenceable slices, but every slice must converge directly on that target rather than accumulating small temporary layers that each pass locally while leaving the product incomplete or structurally duplicated.
6. **Modular internal decomposition, not Alternative C.** The Workspace may compose purpose-specific identity, reference, proposal, request/observation, assessment, lineage and adequacy records/services. This is internal decomposition of one coherent architecture, not several competing mini-architectures or a requirement for one giant Workspace class.
7. **No universal evidence hierarchy is implied.** B3 should prefer stable references/projections over forcing CI, package-manager, upstream, target and impact records into one generic `Evidence` base type.
8. **Research pressures become explicit contract tests, not architecture owners.** B3 must make evidence availability/use states, independent binding/activation/capture propositions, scoped negatives/unknowns, completion-versus-adequacy, failed/empty observations, recommendation admission and payload/annotation identity representable without copying the research harness.
9. **Logical revision/lineage is required; storage/mutability is not selected.** The design must know which investigation/candidate/proposition/request state an observation or evaluation belongs to, but B2 does not select event sourcing, database persistence, mutable objects or immutable snapshots.

### Replacement-oriented migration target

The architectural target is now conceptually:

```text
native producers / domain evaluators
                ↓
       Investigation Workspace
                ↓
   bounded Investigator interaction
                ↓
 proposals / requests / observations
                ↓
      independent evaluation
                ↓
        workspace revision
                ↓
      synthesis / report projection
```

During a later Build, migration may temporarily bridge the current fixed result/report path when concrete proof or caller compatibility requires it, but the bridge is transitional rather than a second destination. The desired end state is that report/synthesis consume the admitted Workspace state or an explicit Workspace-owned projection, while obsolete fixed-snapshot plumbing is removed unless an independent responsibility earns retention.

### Ali's explicit engineering preference clarified at B2

Ali challenged the first recommendation because preserving the old snapshot by default and beginning with an intentionally minimal parallel Workspace risked turning caution into architectural inertia: repeated “minimal” layers can each appear implemented while the end-to-end product remains incomplete, slow to converge, or burdened by duplicate paths. We agree with that concern.

For this project, apply the already accepted [Operating Guide](../OPERATING_GUIDE.md) §4.1 implementation-retention burden and §4.4 product-responsibility/bounded-increment rules with this explicit preference:

- establish the proper coherent target architecture/responsibility before slicing implementation when the design is sufficiently understood;
- use incremental Build to control proof, complexity and risk, **not** to redefine the target downward for convenience;
- require each staged slice to converge directly on the accepted target and to have a clear role in the useful end-to-end system;
- do not accumulate “temporary minimal” layers, adapters or parallel architectures merely because each is locally safe/easy to test;
- treat migration safety and reversibility as proof techniques, not as automatic authority to preserve legacy mechanisms;
- retain old code/paths only when a current admitted responsibility, real compatibility obligation, proof need or material risk independently earns retention, with removal/reassessment triggers for transitional mechanisms.

This preference does not reject bounded/incremental engineering. It rejects **product-shrinking or architecture-fragmenting incrementalism**. A coherent target can still be implemented in multiple properly verified slices.

## B3 — canonical Workspace contract and lifecycle design

B3 defines a **conceptual product contract**, not selected Python class names or serialization schemas. Names below are descriptive working names for responsibilities. A later implementation may split or combine concrete types only if every responsibility, identity/provenance boundary and failure distinction remains inspectable.

### Canonical state boundary

There is one canonical logical investigation state: the **Investigation Workspace**. It is advanced through revision-bound updates and composes native records, proposals, domain-owned assessments, investigation activity and lineage without becoming a generic truth engine.

The Workspace MUST preserve two identity layers:

- an **investigation lineage identity** for the continuing product investigation;
- an exact **target snapshot identity** for each Workspace revision: repository, pull request, base/head revision and the dependency-transition identity relevant to that revision.

A proposal, request, observation, assessment link or consumer view remains historically bound to the exact Workspace/target revision it was created from. Current applicability is additionally governed by the material request/evaluation premise bindings described below: a logical Workspace revision change alone does not prove that an otherwise same-target result is stale.

### Responsibility-bearing conceptual records

| Contract element | Minimum conceptual fields / links | Concrete responsibility and explicit non-responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace revision** | `investigation_identity`, `workspace_revision_identity`, `parent_revision`, exact `target_snapshot`, `change_reason`, triggering record/result references | Orders logical state changes and binds every derived activity to the exact target state. It does not imply event-sourcing or a storage technology. |
| **Native-record binding** | Workspace-local binding identity, native owner/type, exact native record/reference identity, target/scope binding, available provenance/transformation reference, retained-content/recoverability fact, explicit original-payload identity/digest basis when one exists | Makes a native domain record available to cross-domain consumers without copying/reinterpreting its semantics. Host annotations do not change the native identity. |
| **Host annotation** | annotation identity, annotated record/reference, annotation kind/value, host/method identity, Workspace revision | Adds navigation, delivery or host metadata while preserving the original payload identity. If enriched bytes receive their own digest/identity, that new basis is explicit rather than retroactively changing the native record. |
| **Investigator view** | view identity, Workspace revision, investigation/discovery purpose references, exact delivered records/content/references, bounded material available-but-omitted disclosure, offered capability descriptors | Defines what a mechanism was actually shown. It records delivery/current visibility, not examination, truth, support or sufficiency. Omission is never source absence; the disclosure is consumer-bounded, not an exhaustive retained-byte inventory. |
| **Mechanism run** | run identity, view identity, method/model/config identity at the admitted proof boundary, factual procedural termination outcome, emitted proposal/request/stopping-proposal references, explicit examination/use receipts where observable or `unknown` where not | Separates stage/tool/report completion from investigation adequacy. `finish`, interruption or limit exhaustion can end a run while material investigation remains unfinished; unobservable examination must not be reconstructed from citations. |
| **Proposal** | proposal identity/kind, exact candidate/proposition/relationship target, structured proposed meaning, cited native/observation references, producer run/method, basis Workspace revision | Carries a semantic/candidate/source-association suggestion without self-authorizing support, applicability or action. A citation is a proposal input, not proof that the proposition is established. |
| **Proposal admission** | proposal reference, admitted/rejected outcome, structural/reference/scope checks, checked Workspace revision, rejection reason if any | Establishes only that a proposal is well-formed and eligible for evaluation. It MUST NOT perform semantic evaluation or turn an admitted proposal into an assessment. |
| **Evaluation attempt** | exact evaluation subject (proposal/proposition/candidate or adequacy question), evaluator owner/method where admitted, exact evaluation-input projection/material basis references, basis Workspace revision, outcome (`evaluated`, `unsupported`, `failed`, or `stale`), reason/limitations, domain/adequacy assessment reference only when `evaluated` | `unsupported` means no admitted owner/method currently has authority/capability to evaluate that subject; it is not an invented `unresolved` assessment. `failed` is admitted-evaluator execution/processing failure. `stale` means material target/basis no longer applies. |
| **Discovery objective** | objective identity, exact target/Workspace revision, bounded discovery question/horizon/scope, decision relevance, current candidate-discovery coverage/limitations, origin/provenance/known-constraint references | Supports bounded evidence/candidate discovery before a candidate exists without fabricating an unresolved candidate. Observations can produce candidate proposals or scoped negatives; the objective does not self-establish discovery completeness or absence of impact. |
| **Investigation need** | need identity, exact unresolved/conflicted proposition/candidate, current assessment/reference state, discriminating target, decision materiality, origin assessment/evidence references, basis Workspace revision | Represents why more evidence could matter for an existing non-final proposition. It is proposition-relative; unsupported evaluator capability alone does not create one and it does not authorize execution or maintainer recommendation. |
| **Capability request** | request identity, exactly one request basis (`discovery_objective` or `investigation_need`), capability intent, exact requested target/scope/query, material premise/basis references, expected observation meaning/discriminator, basis Workspace/target revision | Proposes one evidence-acquisition action. It does not claim the action is allowed, performed, successful or recommendation-worthy. |
| **Request admission** | request reference, admitted/rejected outcome, host-bound capability/method, exact bound repository/revision/scope, authorization/security/cost/non-mutation constraints, reason | Owns execution eligibility/binding. Rejection is a host/admission outcome, not negative evidence about a proposition/discovery result. |
| **Capability attempt** | attempt identity, admitted-request reference, actual execution identity/scope/revision, procedural outcome, produced observation/native-record/problem references | Distinguishes “we tried” from “we observed useful evidence.” Successful execution can still yield empty, invalid or unusable evidence. |
| **Observation record / attempt problem** | stable observation identity plus exact scope/context for actual observations; distinct problem identity/kind/details for inaccessible/failed/malformed/unusable outcomes | A real empty file or scoped zero-result is an observation about that exact bounded read/search. A failed read, rejected request or malformed result is a problem, not an absence observation. Internal defects remain separately classifiable. |
| **Candidate/proposition lineage link** | predecessor, successor, relationship (`refines`, `supersedes`, or other admitted relation), triggering observation/assessment, reason, effective Workspace revision, affected prior assessments where material | Preserves why knowledge changed instead of silently overwriting candidate/proposition identity. Same-target counterevidence can create a successor assessment without a target-head change. It does not require event sourcing. |
| **Stopping proposal** | attributed proposer/run, basis Workspace revision/scope, claimed stop reason, remaining known non-final states, considered next investigations where available | Preserves the Investigator's stopping recommendation without granting canonical stopping authority. It is distinct from factual run termination and from an admitted adequacy assessment. |
| **Investigation stop/adequacy assessment** | exact candidate/proposition/discovery/investigation scope, basis Workspace revision, remaining material non-final states/coverage limits, considered feasible justified investigation options, `continue`/`stop` outcome, stop reason, admitted owning method/assessment references | Owns canonical whether worthwhile investigation remains, but only when an admitted owner/method actually evaluates the current state. Unsupported/unavailable/failed adequacy evaluation yields no justified-stop conclusion. This remains separate from stage/report completion and later action-relative sufficiency. |

No generic global confidence score, generic `evidence_status`, or universal `Evidence` superclass is required. A concrete implementation must justify every additional field by an owner, consumer, proof obligation or failure distinction.

### Evidence-use relationships are not one lifecycle enum

The completed research pressure is represented by separate relationships:

```text
AVAILABLE
  = a native/observation record is retained or recoverably referenced in this Workspace revision

DELIVERED / CURRENTLY VISIBLE
  = an Investigator/Evaluator view actually contains that record/content/reference

EXAMINED / USED
  = the mechanism run records an observable use/examination receipt;
    otherwise this remains unknown rather than inferred

CITED
  = a proposal/report references the record

EVALUATED SUPPORT
  = an admitted evaluator explicitly considered the record as support/counterevidence
    for an exact proposition

SUFFICIENT
  = the owning evaluator/synthesis responsibility establishes proposition-relative coverage
    or action-relative sufficiency; there is no Workspace-wide `sufficient=true`
```

This prevents the two research errors we specifically observed: available-but-unexamined evidence cannot be called absent, and cited evidence cannot be treated as semantically sufficient merely because it appears in a valid report.

### Truthful bounded omission disclosure

When a view omits retained/recoverable material in a way that could be mistaken for unavailability or absence, its bounded disclosure preserves, as applicable:

- a stable/addressable native binding or retained reference identity when known;
- whether exact content is retained now, recoverable through an admitted reference, or merely externally referenced;
- the content-versus-reference distinction;
- view/selection scope plus material coverage limitations;
- omission reason where known;
- the resolvable relationship when delivered material itself points to content already retained by the Workspace.

This is **not** a global inventory or exhaustive-relevance contract. The host need not enumerate every retained byte or claim it detected every relevant omission. Unknown omission/relevance coverage remains unknown.

### Bounded discovery before candidate formation

Product Decision Model §7.3 already distinguishes candidate-discovery coverage from evidence/path-model coverage, while §11 proposition-directed investigation begins only after a material non-final proposition exists. The Workspace therefore uses a distinct bounded discovery objective before candidate formation rather than manufacturing an unresolved candidate merely to fit `InvestigationNeed`.

Conceptually:

```text
Workspace W1
  exact dependency transition + admitted source/repository scope
  candidate-discovery coverage = explicitly limited/unknown
↓
Discovery objective D1
  bounded question/horizon + decision relevance + known coverage limits
↓
Capability request R1 (basis = D1)
  exact target/scope/query + expected observation meaning
↓
Host admission → attempt → observation/problem
↓
positive source observation may produce a candidate Proposal
OR scoped negative stays scoped
```

A discovery result cannot by itself establish global candidate-discovery completeness, “no impact,” safety or action permission.

### Acquisition flow — HTTPX target-binding example

The known HTTPX case supplies a useful real pressure path: upstream removal evidence and target `TestClient(app)` usage can exist while the exact installed/bound Starlette path remains unresolved; reference Starlette source is not proof of the target's installed version, and an illustrative Docker capture is not current-pipeline capture authority.

A contract-compliant proposition-relative acquisition flow is:

```text
Workspace revision W7
  native HTTPX removal/source records
  + target TestClient usage
  + reference Starlette forwarding source
  + target-binding proposition = unresolved
↓
Investigation need N1
  discriminating target: establish the exact target Starlette binding/path relevant to the PR
↓
Capability request R1 (basis = N1)
  exact-head read/inspection of an admitted dependency/lock/constraint source
  expected meaning: bounded target-binding evidence, not compatibility proof
↓
Host request admission
  bind repository + PR head H1 + allowed path/scope + read-only capability
```

Then materially different outcomes remain different:

- **request rejected** → preserve the admission rejection; no execution occurred and no negative evidence exists;
- **read/acquisition failed** → preserve an attempt problem (`inaccessible`, provider failure, malformed response, etc.); target binding remains unresolved;
- **file/read succeeds but is empty** → preserve a scoped-empty observation for that exact file/read; do not infer “Starlette absent” globally;
- **bounded search returns zero** → preserve query/mode/source/path/coverage limits; zero within that scope does not eliminate other binding paths;
- **valid exact binding evidence obtained** → admit the native domain record through its owner, add a native-record binding, and invoke the correct target-binding/domain evaluator;
- **observation arrives after the PR/head changed from H1 to H2** → retain it under the old target binding as historical evidence and require admitted equivalence/rebinding before current use. Never silently rebase it onto H2.

A successful observation therefore advances knowledge only through:

```text
request admission
→ capability attempt
→ observation/native record
→ identity/scope/meaning validation
→ domain evaluation
→ Workspace revision
```

not:

```text
tool returned something
→ proposition established
```

### Semantic-proposal flow — HTTPX and pytest examples

A semantic proposal does not require an executable request before it can be evaluated.

**HTTPX example:** an Investigator proposes that the target's `TestClient(app)` path is affected by the HTTPX `app=` removal, citing upstream removal text and reference Starlette forwarding source.

1. Proposal admission validates proposal shape, cited-reference existence, exact source scope and basis Workspace revision.
2. The evaluator owner constructs an **evaluation projection** from the Workspace. It is not restricted to proposer citations: it must include the relevant exact target-binding state, activation/capture limitations, material counterevidence and explicit coverage gaps required by that proposition, or declare what it cannot cover.
3. If no admitted evaluator currently owns that API-change/target-binding semantic proposition, create an `EvaluationAttempt(outcome=unsupported, reason=no_admitted_evaluator_or_role)`; do **not** mint a fake `unresolved` `PropositionAssessment` and do not let the proposal become a finding.
4. If an admitted evaluator exists but the exact target binding remains unknown, it may produce its legitimate domain state (for example unresolved/not-established under its own contract) with evidence/coverage limits.
5. If an admitted evaluator exists and a decision-critical evidence premise remains materially unresolved/conflicted, the owning reasoning may create/refine a proposition-relative `InvestigationNeed`. By contrast, `unsupported` evaluator capability **does not** automatically create such a need or trigger repeated acquisition; additional acquisition must have an independently justified discovery objective or another supported proposition-relative need.

**Pytest examples:**

- A proposal assigning a pytest `9.0.0` warning to `9.0.3` can be structurally valid yet semantically refuted/unsupported by the exact release interval. Structural proposal admission therefore cannot stand in for evaluation.
- A proposal that static CI commands or a partial green capture prove pytest runtime execution/compatibility may be evaluated as not established/unresolved when capture authority or exact exercise is missing. That is different from `unsupported evaluation`: the evaluator exists and can judge the proposition, but the evidence does not close it.
- The retained changelog can be **available** while only its root reference was delivered and the content was never examined. The Workspace must preserve that relationship so a mechanism cannot honestly relabel the evidence as unavailable; bounded omission disclosure need not enumerate every retained record, and the mechanism may request/read the retained content.

### Same-target validity, stale-state and update rules

1. Every proposal/request/evaluation/view/attempt retains its original Workspace/target/basis binding for history.
2. A target snapshot change creates a successor target state; earlier results cannot silently apply to the new target without admitted equivalence/rebinding.
3. **A Workspace revision change with the same target does not by itself make a delayed result stale.** A W7 request returning after W8 may be incorporated into a successor current revision only after validating that exact target identity, request capability/scope, material premise/basis records and expected observation meaning remain applicable.
4. If W8 changed only unrelated state, history may truthfully record `requested at W7 → validated against W8 → ingested into W9`. If W8 changed a material premise, source association, candidate identity, scope or capability meaning, retain the result under its original binding and require explicit revalidation/rebinding before current use; otherwise it remains historical/stale for the affected purpose.
5. Native records are not mutated by Workspace annotations; annotation/enrichment lineage is separate.
6. Proposal admission never writes domain truth. Evaluation writes only through the owning evaluator/result contract.
7. New counterevidence on an unchanged target does not mutate an earlier assessment. Preserve the earlier evidence/assessment, add the new evidence, invoke the owning evaluator and create a successor current assessment; genuine normalized contradiction may become `conflicted` through that owner.
8. An evaluated successor proposition/candidate does not erase the predecessor; lineage explains refinement/supersession and which prior assessments no longer apply.
9. A report or synthesis projection cannot write facts back into the Workspace merely because it rendered them. A later explicit continuation may create new proposals/requests through the normal admission path.

### Canonical stopping and investigation adequacy

The Workspace preserves three separate meanings:

```text
Investigator stopping proposal
!= procedural run termination
!= canonical stop/adequacy assessment
```

An Investigator may propose stopping and a run may factually `finish`, be interrupted or exhaust its limits. Neither establishes adequacy. Canonical `continue`/`stop` exists only when an **admitted investigation-adequacy owner/method** evaluates the exact current scope, remaining material non-final states/coverage obligations and feasible justified investigations under explicit conditions.

If no admitted adequacy method exists, preserve an `EvaluationAttempt(outcome=unsupported)` for the adequacy subject and the remaining open discovery/investigation obligations; do not manufacture justified stop. If an admitted method fails, preserve `failed`; if its material basis changes, its prior assessment becomes historical/stale until reevaluated. The contract does not select whether the future method is deterministic, model-assisted, human-reviewed or otherwise and does not require another LLM/critic.

### Consumer projections from the canonical Workspace

All consumers receive **one-way projections from the canonical Workspace**. Projection may select/reformat information but cannot silently strengthen it.

| Consumer projection | Must contain | Must not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Investigator projection** | exact target/workspace revision; relevant native/observation records with content/reference at admitted retention strength; current proposals/assessments; bounded discovery objectives and proposition-relative open investigation needs; capability descriptors; bounded truthful omission disclosure where material | grant domain truth/action authority; imply omitted evidence is absent; force every available record into the context window or claim exhaustive omission detection |
| **Evaluator projection** | exact evaluation subject; target revision/material basis; proposer citations; owner-selected relevant evidence/counterevidence/unknowns; scope/association/use/coverage information; evaluator method identity | limit evaluation to what the proposer chose to cite; silently manufacture unavailable evidence; conflate support with source existence |
| **Synthesis projection** | exact decision identity; domain-owned evaluated candidates/propositions; candidate-discovery coverage limits; residual unknowns/conflicts; material failed/unsupported/stale acquisition/evaluation/adequacy outcomes; canonical investigation stopping state **where established**; provenance/coverage limits; repository context actually owned | treat raw proposals as permission; manufacture discovery completeness; convert procedural finish/unsupported adequacy into investigation stop; convert missing evaluator/capability into an action without Synthesis's own positive permission |
| **Report projection** | the synthesis/action result plus material evidence links, findings, unknowns, limitations and projection/version/retention metadata | become canonical state; establish new semantics; claim resumability/replay from missing inputs |
| **Recovery projection/checkpoint** | one coherent declared canonical revision, its material evidence/history/method dependencies, retention gaps and unfinished work; the minimum recovery boundary proposed below | claim report prose or absent history is resumable/replayable state; automatically execute, retry or rebase recovered work |
| **Temporary legacy `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` projection, only if independently justified during migration** | only meanings that can be losslessly/intentionally projected from the canonical Workspace/native owners, with explicit compatibility/version limits | run a second acquisition/orchestration path, become a second source of truth, or earn permanent retention merely because old tests/callers exist |

### Replacement-oriented migration completion conditions

Migration from the current fixed snapshot is complete only when all applicable conditions below hold:

1. **Canonical production path:** normal bounded discovery/investigation constructs/advances the Workspace directly from admitted native producers/evaluators; no parallel `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` path independently acquires or interprets the same facts.
2. **Current-semantic coverage:** every currently admitted fact/assessment needed by product behavior/reporting has a native binding or intentional Workspace projection with no silent authority loss/strengthening. Intentional contract changes are explicit rather than hidden as “migration.”
3. **Consumer cutover:** CLI/orchestration, maintainer synthesis and report projection consume Workspace-owned projections rather than treating the old snapshot as canonical input.
4. **Legacy bridge direction:** if a temporary `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` adapter is still needed, it is derived one-way **from the canonical Workspace** and has an independently justified compatibility/proof owner plus removal/reassessment trigger.
5. **Behavior/proof parity plus intentional deltas:** current deterministic regression/report behavior and representative real-case semantics are verified through the Workspace path; new B3 obligations additionally cover pre-candidate discovery versus proposition-directed follow-up, proposal admission versus support, unsupported semantic/adequacy evaluation, failed/empty observations, bounded omission/evidence-use truth, same-target delayed results, counterevidence reevaluation and target-changing staleness.
6. **No duplicate truth/evaluator ownership:** domain facts, proposition assessments and any canonical adequacy assessments have one authoritative owner; the Workspace and compatibility projections reference/project rather than recompute competing semantics.
7. **Report versus recovery separation:** saved report opening remains an offline projection operation. Durable canonical Workspace recovery is an intended product responsibility under the B4 conclusion below; later migration acceptance must prove the reviewed recovery contract or explicitly preserve its staged implementation debt, rather than calling a report or obsolete snapshot resumable state.
8. **Retirement decision executed:** when no independent compatibility/external obligation remains, remove `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` and its dedicated normal-path plumbing/tests rather than keeping a dormant second architecture. If a real external compatibility obligation survives, version and bound that adapter explicitly.
9. **Documentation and tests name the canonical boundary:** active source comments, tests and user/developer-facing documentation stop teaching the old snapshot as the product investigation contract once cutover is complete.

### Explicit persistence/recovery-responsibility comparison

| Alternative | Product consequence / burden | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saved reports only; restart investigation | Lowest retention burden but loses objectives/needs, failure/evaluation lineage, retained context and interrupted-work distinctions. Reacquisition may not recover original evidence. | Rejected as the full intended responsibility. Report-only operation can be an explicitly incomplete implementation stage. |

| Deterministic replay from retained captured/normalized inputs | Re-executes an admitted processing boundary with recorded inputs/methods and an explicit equivalence oracle, independently of live sources. | Unrestricted full execution replay, including every model/tool/process state | Much greater capture/privacy/compatibility burden; visible inputs do not guarantee identical external sources/model output. **Conclusion: yes, durable canonical Workspace recovery is intended.** This defines the Workspace recovery responsibility within the persisted-run outcome; it does **not** concretize or satisfy Charter §6's separate deterministic replay obligation. No database or implementation is activated and no durability/replay behavior is established. The new recovery semantic boundary is proposed for Core below; result review/acceptance remains separate from this assistant's recommendation. ### Minimum recovery semantics — proposed Core §6.4 delta for review

> **6.4 Canonical investigation recovery and explicit continuation**
>
> When canonical investigation recovery is activated, a durable checkpoint MUST declare one coherent investigation boundary: lineage, exact repository/PR/base/head/dependency transition, canonical revision and supported representation/semantic-version identity. Recovery MUST identify the persistence boundary; it MUST NOT imply preservation of later uncheckpointed activity. Checkpoint cadence and retention duration require an explicit implementation contract before durability is claimed.
>
> The checkpoint MUST preserve the material dependency closure needed to explain and continue that boundary: native identity/owner/scope/provenance and retained content or explicit recovery limitations; material proposal/admission and evaluation inputs/results; candidate/proposition lineage and reasons; discovery objectives/coverage; investigation needs; request/admission/attempt/observation/problem relationships; procedural termination, stopping proposals and adequacy assessments or unsupported/failed outcomes. Material views and available method/model/configuration identity MUST be retained at their admitted proof boundary, with missing provenance explicit. Reference-only content MUST NOT be labeled exact retained evidence. No universal raw capture, hidden model reasoning or secret retention is required or authorized.
>
> Recovery MUST preserve authority, assessment basis, open work and failed/unsupported/stale/conflicted distinctions. It MUST NOT convert proposals into assessments, omitted evidence into absence, ambiguous attempts into successes/negative observations, or procedural completion into adequate investigation. Completed observations absent from the declared boundary MUST NOT be invented.
>
> Supported representation, internal identities/references and integrity/coherence MUST be validated before recovered state is presented as canonical. Torn/invalid boundaries MUST NOT be silently merged or repaired into authoritative truth. A separately validated earlier boundary MAY be restored with explicit lost-progress limits. Incompatible versions, broken material references, missing required content and integrity problems MUST have explicit outcomes. Readable historical material MAY remain inspectable with declared limits; it MUST NOT be advertised as fully resumable where gaps block the next obligation.
>
> Recovery MUST be offline historical restoration. It MUST NOT issue requests, invoke models/evaluators, execute content, retry work, rebase targets or infer present source validity. Offline report opening remains a separate projection operation and MUST NOT reconstruct absent canonical state.
>
> Explicit continuation MUST distinguish fresh facts and state changes from the checkpoint. Before using recovered records/requests/assessments as current, validate the exact target and material identity/premise/scope/method/authority bindings; logical revision inequality alone is not staleness. Changed targets cannot be silently rebound. Missing methods/capabilities and material retention gaps MUST remain explicit and block affected operations without discarding unaffected evidence or manufacturing assessments.
>
> Pending, interrupted and completion-unknown attempts MUST remain distinguishable from unperformed requests and completed observations. Reconciliation/retry requires explicit continuation and current host admission under the capability's effects/authorization contract. Historical admission MUST NOT independently grant present execution authority or imply exactly-once execution. Duplicate results MUST be identified against request/attempt/observation identity and checked for contradictory content before ingestion.
>
> Recovered adequacy/synthesis conclusions MUST retain their historical basis. Material new evidence, discovery obligations, target/premise changes or newly feasible decision-critical checks require validity review and affected successor evaluation by the admitted owner before current use. Recovery cannot self-authorize stop/action; absent current adequacy/evaluation methods preserve unsupported evaluation rather than justified stop.
>
> Recovery promises MUST be evidenced through interruption, coherent restoration, missing/corrupted/incompatible dependencies, ambiguous attempts, target change and explicit continuation tests. Recovery does not discharge the separate replay responsibility: replay requires its own admitted re-execution boundary, retained inputs/method identities, equivalence criteria and executable proof independently of live source availability. Recovery does not establish deterministic replay, semantic correctness, current compatibility, discovery completeness or action permission.

**Minimum does not mean report-sized:** retain the material content/history needed by the named continuation/evaluation consumer, rather than only final answers. Unrelated transient transport/debug bytes may be discarded with justification; a material source input cannot be hidden behind its citation/digest. Exact separately retained content is allowed when its identity/availability and recovery boundary are validated. Failed external reacquisition leaves explicit loss; newly retrieved bytes cannot overwrite what was originally supplied.

### Cross-case consolidation and structural promotion
