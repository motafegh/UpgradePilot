### B1 genuine missing responsibilities

The source trace does **not** reveal a missing universal evidence type or a need to replace current domain owners. It reveals a cross-domain product gap:

- one evolving investigation identity/current-state composition owner across native records, proposals, evaluated propositions/candidates, missing premises and relevant history;
- explicit references/lineage from derived records and conclusions back to exact native inputs and method identity, without forcing all domain evidence into one base class;
- general proposal and capability-request representation, host-owned binding/eligibility/admission, and separate execution/observation/failure records;
- observation ingestion and knowledge-update rules, including stale-input/revision handling and candidate/proposition refinement lineage;
- bounded Investigator views that distinguish what exists from what was delivered/examined/cited/evaluated as support;
- recoverable retained investigation state distinct from the human/saved report projection;
- an interface by which synthesis consumes evaluated knowledge while retaining separate recommendation/action authority.

**Adapter rule carried into B2:** add an adapter only when a cross-domain consumer genuinely needs a stable reference/projection that a native owner does not already provide. An adapter must preserve the native record’s identity, scope, provenance and limitations; it must not reinterpret or duplicate the domain fact merely for workspace uniformity.

**B1 conclusion:** the existing product is not missing a generic “reasoning core.” Its strongest current property is the separation of domain-owned evidence/evaluation boundaries. B2 should therefore compare composition approaches by how well they preserve those owners while adding evolving cross-domain state. This finding favors composition/reference over duplication, but it does not yet choose among the B2 alternatives.

## B2 — accepted workspace composition decision

B2 compared three product-level composition shapes. The comparison deliberately does **not** choose concrete classes, storage, mutability, event sourcing, Investigator policy, model/tool topology or evaluator implementation. The decision is where evolving cross-domain investigation responsibility belongs while current domain owners remain authoritative.

### Evaluation criteria

The alternatives were evaluated against: (1) native authority separation and typed-record retention; (2) compatibility/migration pressure from the current fixed investigation/report path; (3) evolving state, history, candidate/proposition lineage and stale-state handling; (4) evidence-use/derivation lineage and the seven completed Contract-2 pressures; (5) request/observation/failure lifecycle and investigation-adequacy representation; (6) bounded Investigator views and omission-versus-absence truth; (7) original-payload/host-annotation identity clarity; (8) migration/reversibility; and (9) risk of accidental generic truth ownership, architectural sprawl or indefinite legacy layering.

| Alternative | Strengths | Material problems | B2 disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Extend `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` into the evolving workspace** | Reuses the current application result and gives existing consumers one familiar object; initially appears to minimize new top-level concepts. | The current type is explicitly a frozen result of a fixed read-only sequence. Adding proposals, requests, observations, history, delivery/examination state, stage adequacy, annotations and revisions would combine snapshot/result semantics with process/lifecycle semantics. Optional-field growth and adaptive updates would make the fixed-sequence result increasingly misleading. Satisfying B2/B3 requirements would effectively turn the existing result into a differently purposed workspace under the old name. | **Rejected.** Do not evolve this type into the workspace. Existing consumers create migration work, not automatic authority to keep this result as a permanent first-class contract. |
| **B — Introduce a distinct composed/evolving Investigation Workspace around native records** | Cleanly separates domain-owned facts from cross-domain composition/lifecycle responsibility. Can retain/reference native typed records without flattening them; can own proposals, assessments, missing premises, requests, observations/failures, lineage/history, bounded views and adequacy metadata. It can express all seven research pressures without making the Investigator or report the truth owner. | A new workspace can become a “god object” or generic evidence warehouse if it copies/reinterprets native facts. It introduces a consequential central cross-module contract that requires disciplined interfaces and likely an ADR. | **Accepted structural direction.** The Workspace is intended to become the canonical product investigation state/contract. Its ownership is composition/lifecycle, not domain truth or action permission. |
| **C — Add only a smaller cross-domain state/update owner and keep the rest distributed** | Lowest apparent initial migration cost and highlights the value of small focused internals. | The accepted requirements are broader than a current-state map: investigation identity, request/observation/failure history, proposal/evaluation lineage, bounded delivered/examined views, adequacy/stopping, annotation identity and recoverable state all require a coherent lifecycle boundary. Splitting these into ad hoc top-level owners risks inconsistent revision identity and omission/absence semantics; growing them enough to solve the problem recreates B indirectly. | **Rejected as a competing architecture.** Its useful lesson survives only as modular internal decomposition: the canonical Workspace should compose focused records/services instead of becoming one monolithic class. |

### Accepted structural direction

Ali explicitly accepted **Alternative B: a distinct composed/evolving Investigation Workspace**, after challenging the originally over-cautious migration proposal. The accepted constraints carried into B3 are:

1. **Composition, not reinterpretation.** Native provider/domain records and evaluator results remain authoritative. The Workspace retains or references them with identity, scope, provenance and limitations; it does not manufacture generic replacements merely for uniformity.
2. **Lifecycle owner, not truth owner.** The Workspace owns investigation identity/revision, cross-domain relationships, proposal/request/observation/evaluation lineage, missing-premise state, bounded views and history/recovery semantics. Domain evaluators still decide domain propositions; maintainer synthesis still owns action-relative output.
3. **Canonical target, not a permanent parallel experiment.** The Workspace is intended to become the product's canonical investigation state/contract. `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` is existing architecture to migrate from, not a second long-term product path that must survive beside the Workspace.
4. **Replacement-oriented migration.** Migration safety is used to preserve proof and reversibility while moving to the accepted target. A temporary snapshot adapter/projection may exist only when a concrete caller/proof/compatibility need justifies it, and its removal/reassessment condition must be explicit. Current consumers and passing tests establish migration pressure; they do not independently justify permanent retention.
5. **No stack of “minimal” architectures.** Design the coherent target responsibility first. Later implementation may be split into evidenceable slices, but every slice must converge directly on that target rather than accumulating small temporary layers that each pass locally while leaving the product incomplete or structurally duplicated.
6. **Modular internal decomposition, not Alternative C.** The Workspace may compose purpose-specific identity, reference, proposal, request/observation, assessment, lineage and adequacy records/services. This is internal decomposition of one coherent architecture, not several competing mini-architectures or a requirement for one giant Workspace class.
7. **No universal evidence hierarchy is implied.** B3 should prefer stable references/projections over forcing CI, package-manager, upstream, target and impact records into one generic `Evidence` base type.
8. **Research pressures become explicit contract tests, not architecture owners.** B3 must make evidence availability/use states, independent binding/activation/capture propositions, scoped negatives/unknowns, completion-versus-adequacy, failed/empty observations, recommendation admission and payload/annotation identity representable without copying the research harness.
9. **Logical revision/lineage is required; storage/mutability is not selected.** The design must know which investigation/candidate/proposition/request state an observation or evaluation belongs to, but B2 does not select event sourcing, database persistence, mutable objects or immutable snapshots.

### Replacement-oriented migration target

The architectural target is now conceptually:

```text
native producers / domain evaluators
                ↓
       Investigation Workspace
                ↓
   bounded Investigator interaction
                ↓
 proposals / requests / observations
                ↓
      independent evaluation
                ↓
        workspace revision
                ↓
      synthesis / report projection
```

During a later Build, migration may temporarily bridge the current fixed result/report path when concrete proof or caller compatibility requires it, but the bridge is transitional rather than a second destination. The desired end state is that report/synthesis consume the admitted Workspace state or an explicit Workspace-owned projection, while obsolete fixed-snapshot plumbing is removed unless an independent responsibility earns retention.

### Ali's explicit engineering preference clarified at B2

Ali challenged the first recommendation because preserving the old snapshot by default and beginning with an intentionally minimal parallel Workspace risked turning caution into architectural inertia: repeated “minimal” layers can each appear implemented while the end-to-end product remains incomplete, slow to converge, or burdened by duplicate paths. We agree with that concern.

For this project, apply the already accepted [Operating Guide](../OPERATING_GUIDE.md) §4.1 implementation-retention burden and §4.4 product-responsibility/bounded-increment rules with this explicit preference:

- establish the proper coherent target architecture/responsibility before slicing implementation when the design is sufficiently understood;
- use incremental Build to control proof, complexity and risk, **not** to redefine the target downward for convenience;
- require each staged slice to converge directly on the accepted target and to have a clear role in the useful end-to-end system;
- do not accumulate “temporary minimal” layers, adapters or parallel architectures merely because each is locally safe/easy to test;
- treat migration safety and reversibility as proof techniques, not as automatic authority to preserve legacy mechanisms;
- retain old code/paths only when a current admitted responsibility, real compatibility obligation, proof need or material risk independently earns retention, with removal/reassessment triggers for transitional mechanisms.

This preference does not reject bounded/incremental engineering. It rejects **product-shrinking or architecture-fragmenting incrementalism**. A coherent target can still be implemented in multiple properly verified slices.

## A2 gate closure

Ali explicitly accepted the orientation and supplied his own reasoning at the pre-B gate:

- A passing CI result or any other isolated evidence/subset must not self-authorize a compatibility/final conclusion. The proper decision/evaluation owner must reason over the relevant evidence boundary, and a conclusion must preserve reasons and provenance rather than reduce to unsupported prose such as “CI passed, therefore compatible.”

Availability alone must not be confused with actual use.
