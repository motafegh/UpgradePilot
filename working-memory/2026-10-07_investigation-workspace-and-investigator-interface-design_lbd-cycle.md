### B1 genuine missing responsibilities

The source trace does **not** reveal a missing universal evidence type or a need to replace current domain owners. It reveals a cross-domain product gap:

- one evolving investigation identity/current-state composition owner across native records, proposals, evaluated propositions/candidates, missing premises and relevant history;
- explicit references/lineage from derived records and conclusions back to exact native inputs and method identity, without forcing all domain evidence into one base class;
- general proposal and capability-request representation, host-owned binding/eligibility/admission, and separate execution/observation/failure records;
- observation ingestion and knowledge-update rules, including stale-input/revision handling and candidate/proposition refinement lineage;
- bounded Investigator views that distinguish what exists from what was delivered/examined/cited/evaluated as support;
- recoverable retained investigation state distinct from the human/saved report projection;
- an interface by which synthesis consumes evaluated knowledge while retaining separate recommendation/action authority.

**Adapter rule carried into B2:** add an adapter only when a cross-domain consumer genuinely needs a stable reference/projection that a native owner does not already provide. An adapter must preserve the native record’s identity, scope, provenance and limitations; it must not reinterpret or duplicate the domain fact merely for workspace uniformity.

**B1 conclusion:** the existing product is not missing a generic “reasoning core.” Its strongest current property is the separation of domain-owned evidence/evaluation boundaries. B2 should therefore compare composition approaches by how well they preserve those owners while adding evolving cross-domain state. This finding favors composition/reference over duplication, but it does not yet choose among the B2 alternatives.

## B2 — workspace composition alternatives at review checkpoint

B2 compares three product-level composition shapes. The comparison deliberately does **not** choose concrete classes, storage, mutability, event sourcing, Investigator policy, model/tool topology or evaluator implementation. The question is where evolving cross-domain investigation responsibility should live while current domain owners remain authoritative.

### Evaluation criteria

The alternatives are evaluated against: (1) native authority separation and typed-record retention; (2) compatibility with the current fixed investigation/report path; (3) evolving state, history, candidate/proposition lineage and stale-state handling; (4) evidence-use/derivation lineage and the seven completed Contract-2 pressures; (5) request/observation/failure lifecycle and investigation-adequacy representation; (6) bounded Investigator views and omission-versus-absence truth; (7) original-payload/host-annotation identity clarity; (8) migration/reversibility; and (9) risk of accidental generic truth ownership or architectural sprawl.

| Alternative | Strengths | Material problems | B2 disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Extend `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` into the evolving workspace** | Reuses the current application result and gives existing consumers one familiar object; initially appears to minimize new top-level concepts. | The current type is explicitly a frozen result of a fixed read-only sequence. Adding proposals, requests, observations, history, delivery/examination state, stage adequacy, annotations and revisions would combine snapshot/result semantics with process/lifecycle semantics. Optional-field growth and adaptive updates would make the fixed-sequence result increasingly misleading. Report consumers currently expect the snapshot shape; making it the mutable/evolving authority couples migration of investigation architecture to presentation consumers. Satisfying B2/B3 requirements would effectively turn the existing result into a differently purposed workspace under the old name. | **Reject as the primary workspace direction.** Preserve it as the current fixed snapshot/compatibility boundary unless a later migration proves replacement is justified. |
| **B — Introduce a distinct composed/evolving Investigation Workspace around native records** | Cleanly separates domain-owned facts from cross-domain composition/lifecycle responsibility. Can retain/reference native typed records without flattening them; can own proposals, assessments, missing premises, requests, observations/failures, lineage/history, bounded views and adequacy metadata. It can express all seven research pressures without making the Investigator or report the truth owner. Existing `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` and report/synthesis consumers can remain intact during an incremental migration. | A new workspace can become a “god object” or generic evidence warehouse if it copies/reinterprets native facts. It also introduces a new central cross-module contract that requires disciplined interfaces and likely an ADR. These risks must be constrained in B3 by composition/reference rules, explicit owner boundaries and small purpose-specific records/services rather than a universal evidence superclass. | **Preferred structural direction, pending Ali review.** Workspace owns composition/lifecycle, not domain truth. Current snapshot/report path remains available during migration. |
| **C — Add only a smaller cross-domain state/update owner and keep the rest distributed** | Lowest apparent migration cost; encourages a small core and could avoid one large aggregate. It is attractive if the missing responsibility were only a current-state map. | The accepted requirements are broader than a current-state map: investigation identity, request/observation/failure history, proposal/evaluation lineage, bounded delivered/examined views, adequacy/stopping, annotation identity and recoverable state all require a coherent lifecycle boundary. Keeping these responsibilities in separate ad hoc owners risks inconsistent revision identity and omission/absence semantics. If C grows enough adjacent ledgers/controllers to satisfy them coherently, it converges conceptually on B. | **Do not select as a separate top-level architecture.** Preserve its minimality as an implementation/decomposition principle inside B: the workspace can compose smaller ledgers/services rather than become one giant record. |

### Preferred structural direction

Subject to Ali's review checkpoint, B2 recommends **Alternative B: a distinct composed/evolving Investigation Workspace**, with these structural constraints carried into B3:

1. **Composition, not reinterpretation.** Native provider/domain records and evaluator results remain authoritative. The workspace retains or references them with identity, scope, provenance and limitations; it does not manufacture generic replacements merely for uniformity.
2. **Lifecycle owner, not truth owner.** The workspace owns investigation identity/revision, cross-domain relationships, proposal/request/observation/evaluation lineage, missing-premise state, bounded views and history/recovery semantics. Domain evaluators still decide domain propositions; maintainer synthesis still owns action-relative output.
3. **Keep the existing snapshot path initially.** `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` remains the fixed current result/integration boundary. Existing report projection can continue consuming it while the workspace is introduced alongside or bootstrapped from the same native outputs. No immediate report/synthesis migration is required merely to establish the workspace contract.
4. **Incremental and reversible migration.** A first Build may populate workspace state from current native outputs while preserving the old result/report path. Later consumers can move only when the workspace projection is proven equivalent or intentionally richer. If the workspace experiment fails, the current deterministic path remains intact.
5. **No universal evidence hierarchy is implied.** B3 should prefer stable references/projections over forcing CI, package-manager, upstream, target and impact records into one generic `Evidence` base type.
6. **Research pressures become explicit contract tests, not architecture owners.** B3 must make evidence availability/use states, independent binding/activation/capture propositions, scoped negatives/unknowns, completion-versus-adequacy, failed/empty observations, recommendation admission and payload/annotation identity representable without copying the research harness.
7. **Logical revision/lineage is required; storage/mutability is not selected.** The design must know which investigation/candidate/proposition/request state an observation or evaluation belongs to, but B2 does not select event sourcing, database persistence, mutable objects or immutable snapshots.

### Migration shape if B is accepted

The least disruptive path is conceptually:

```text
existing native producers/evaluators
            ├──────────────→ PublicPullRequestInvestigation → current synthesis/report
            │
            └──────────────→ Investigation Workspace composition/update
                                  ↓
                          bounded Investigator view
                                  ↓
                    proposals / requests / observations
                                  ↓
                        independent evaluation
                                  ↓
                         workspace revision
```

A later reviewed migration may project report/synthesis inputs from the workspace, but that is not required to start the workspace and is not selected in B2. This preserves current consumers while letting B3 define the new contract cleanly.

## A2 gate closure

Ali explicitly accepted the orientation and supplied his own reasoning at the pre-B gate:

- A passing CI result or any other isolated evidence/subset must not self-authorize a compatibility/final conclusion. The proper decision/evaluation owner must reason over the relevant evidence boundary, and a conclusion must preserve reasons and provenance rather than reduce to unsupported prose such as “CI passed, therefore compatible.”
- Evidence use must be traceable. The system should record which evidence supported a conclusion and, where one evidence/result was derived from other evidence, preserve that derivation relationship so later diagnosis, audit and debugging can reconstruct the path. Availability alone must not be confused with actual use.
- The distinction between the current fixed-sequence `PublicPullRequestInvestigation` result and a possible evolving workspace is understood. No premature combination, expansion or replacement is selected; B owns the actual alternative comparison.

This clears the canonical A2 pre-B understanding gate. It does not select a workspace architecture, evaluator, model, framework, persistence method or Investigator policy.
