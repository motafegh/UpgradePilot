## Learner-owned distinctions

- Mechanical/interface validation checks whether the model output obeys the wire/report contract and references legitimate, visible, currently valid evidence.
- Semantic validation is separate: it asks whether the cited/available evidence actually supports, contradicts, or leaves unresolved the model-authored proposition.
- A valid citation therefore does not make a claim semantically valid.
- Model-authored notes/recommendations remain attributed interpretation; rereading them does not convert them into independent source corroboration.
- `not provided` and `not inspected` are different states: the former is evidence availability; the latter is investigation/coverage. Confusing them can hide a stopping/navigation failure as a source limitation.
- Decision authority remains downstream of evidence, interpretation and semantic validation; an Investigator report is not action permission.
