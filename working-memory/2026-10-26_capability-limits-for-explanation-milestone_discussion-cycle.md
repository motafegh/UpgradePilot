## Decision-relevant model and initial prioritization proposals

Start with the missing proposition, then identify its owner:

1. **Needed source was never acquired:** source/context qualification or acquisition responsibility. More reasoning over absent data does not supply it.
2. **Relevant source exists but is interpreted incorrectly or its relationship is unestablished:** extraction, correspondence and interpretation responsibility. Another capture alone does not repair that failure.
3. **A stronger runtime conclusion is requested:** execution/environment evidence. Static source can establish a conditional structural relation without establishing installed state or actual behavior.
