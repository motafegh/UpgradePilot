## Audit question

For the current UpgradePilot product and retained evidence, determine:

1. what the Charter actually requires the product to prove beyond the transparent baseline;
2. what current implemented output can already be fairly evaluated;
3. whether the prepared product-value feasibility plan asks the right question with the right case universe and proof boundaries;
4. which retained simulations/experiments can support development comparison versus which are contaminated, out of scope, incomplete or non-independent;
5. whether current Investigator/LLM directions are justified by a residual product-value question or are premature;
6. what the smallest credible next evaluation/increment is, including explicit reject/defer/stop options.

## Boundaries

- audit first; do not run benchmark arms, model inference, live acquisition or target execution;
- historical simulation and experiment outputs remain immutable;
- exposed development cases do not become protected validation merely because they were not used in one recent experiment;
- product-value, semantic adequacy, action permission and independent maintainer usefulness are separate claims;
- current explained abstention may have information value but does not by itself demonstrate superior actionable recommendation;
- more citations, stages, agents or model calls are not value metrics.
