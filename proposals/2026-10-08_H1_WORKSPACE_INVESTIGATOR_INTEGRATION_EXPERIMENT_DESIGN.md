# H1 — product-shaped single-Investigator integration and experiment proposal

**Status:** Candidate for joint review; design only. **Recorded:** 2026-10-08. No H1 implementation, execution, unseen-case selection, new model, critic topology, framework or action-authority change is admitted by this document.

**Responsibility:** Evaluate whether structured product Workspace views/fact capabilities improve a single adaptive Investigator over raw navigation. Main owns Workspace composition/lifecycle, native domain truth, admission, evaluation and product projections. Research owns treatment/control design, instrumentation and mechanism adjudication. This proposal defines no alternative canonical Workspace, Python types, storage schema or product API.

**Question:** Does a single adaptive Investigator operating against structured UpgradePilot Workspace/fact capabilities improve relevant evidence coverage, correct evidence use, applicability reasoning, uncertainty preservation and stopping compared with the raw-navigation Agent?

Discovery advantages vary. Delivered evidence can still be ignored or interpreted wrongly. Separate interpretation-only evidence establishes that retrieval is not the sole observed bottleneck; it is not pooled into H1 or treated as model incapability.

Main B4/recovery/migration work and proposed ADR-0012 were uncommitted at initial inspection. During design verification, main published B4 at `43b9b470` and the recovery/replay clarification at `08243e5b3e455ad26ae1cd142763f4d147269bc6`; [ADR-0012](https://github.com/motafegh/UpgradePilot/blob/08243e5b3e455ad26ae1cd142763f4d147269bc6/docs/architecture/ADR-0012-canonical-investigation-workspace-and-recovery-boundary.md) remains proposed for Ali's review, and the Core recovery delta is not yet accepted. No executable interface changed. These are recorded dependencies, not a replacement H1 interface/API freeze. The current Charter distinguishes persisted recovery from separately required deterministic retained-input replay; H1 establishes neither. [Baseline manifest](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-08-h1-integration-design/baseline-manifest.json) pins the inspected owners and source.

Accepted [Product Decision Model](../docs/specifications/UPGRADEPILOT_PRODUCT_DECISION_MODEL_SPECIFICATION.md) §§7,9–13 owns coverage, identity, authority, proposition-directed investigation, validity and stopping; [Core](../docs/specifications/UPGRADEPILOT_CORE_PIPELINE_AND_CONTRACT_SPECIFICATION.md) §§6.1–6.3 owns representation/consumer/source-association boundaries; [Minimum Useful Generality](../docs/specifications/UPGRADEPILOT_MINIMUM_USEFUL_GENERALITY_SPECIFICATION.md) prevents case-specific source selection or answer rules. Main retains [Maintainer Action Synthesis](../docs/specifications/UPGRADEPILOT_MAINTAINER_ACTION_SYNTHESIS_SPECIFICATION.md) authority.

The full product responsibility includes broad discovery, evaluated applicability, independent adequacy, synthesis and prospective recovery. H1's proposed slice evaluates one Investigator's discovery/proposal/request/evidence-use/stop behavior using the canonical interface. It does not implement general semantic or adequacy evaluation, recovery/migration or deterministic replay, all impact mechanisms, compatibility proof or product action permission. Those omissions are temporary integration/evaluation boundaries, not reductions of product scope. Expansion requires main-owned capabilities/contracts and separate experimental selection.

This is an experiment's consumption requirement on B3, not a replacement product contract. Conceptual field names are taken from main; concrete identifiers, methods, wire schemas and storage remain main decisions.

|---|---|---|

| Relevant native knowledge | Stable binding/owner/type, exact payload/reference at retained strength, provenance/association/capture authority, native state and limitations. | Native owner plus main projection. Group by product responsibility, without flattening facts into a generic confidence/status. |
| Proposed versus evaluated state | Attributed candidates/semantic proposals, structural admission, evaluator-attempt outcome and actual assessment link only when evaluated. | Main/evaluator. No self-evaluated proposal. Unsupported role and real unresolved domain state remain different. |
| Availability and delivery | Exact content/references delivered in this view; materially addressable retained/recoverable but omitted content; content-versus-reference and omission/selection limitations. | Main. Bounded disclosure, no exhaustive relevance inventory or inferred model understanding. |

**Proposed capability responsibilities** (labels below are not selected API/function names):

1. Read a bounded Workspace projection/history page or inspect an addressable binding. This provides state/navigation, not acquisition or new truth. Projection/read access must still be scoped by purpose; main should clarify whether it needs capability-attempt records or a distinct view-delivery route. Do not force pure view delivery into a fake evidence acquisition.
2. Request exact frozen source listing/read/literal search/capture inspection. Preserve current paging/scope behavior, empty-file and zero-result observations, problems and available-reference resolution. All acquisition requests cite exactly one admitted DiscoveryObjective or InvestigationNeed.
3. Request/read an existing native fact-family projection, or invoke an offered native producer on its actually sufficient bound inputs. Distinguish projection of an existing record from a producer attempt; neither route invents missing prerequisites or source authority. Response preserves source refs/counterevidence/unknown limits, not a case answer.
4. Submit candidate/semantic/source-association/refinement proposals for main's structural admission and potential evaluator routing. Candidate discovery is a proposal until admitted/evaluated through appropriate ownership; self-reported uncertainty remains attributed.
5. Propose a bounded discovery objective or proposition-relative next investigation for host/domain admission where main permits that origin. This does not create a candidate/non-final assessment or an executable request by itself.
6. Submit an attributed stopping proposal and finish the mechanism run. State remaining known obligations/gaps and rationale; canonical adequacy is a separate attempt/assessment.

There is no unrestricted filesystem/shell, target-code execution, live PR mutation, merge/check recommendation permission, automatic retry, model-selected evaluator or critic. Native capability evaluation may run within its existing authority; no additional LLM is invoked. Relevant source fallback remains available to H1 so the experiment does not test a hand-curated fact packet only.

### Independent evaluator consumption

Main's evaluator projection must carry the exact subject/target/material basis, proposer citations **and** owner-selected relevant counterevidence/unknowns and coverage limits. H1 cannot constrain evaluation to its chosen citations. Research measures these inputs and outcomes when native evaluation exists; it does not choose a semantic evaluator implementation. No admitted general API evaluator or adequacy method means an unsupported evaluation attempt, with no fabricated authoritative unresolved assessment. Offline source review cannot silently populate runtime assessments.

## 4. Discovery, follow-up, proposals and stopping

**Pre-candidate path:** shared target/transition/inventory → bounded DiscoveryObjective → scoped capability request → host admission → actual attempt → observation/problem → candidate/semantic proposal. Initial objectives use generic update-relevant questions/horizons and supplied constraints, not known HTTPX/pytest answer paths. Scoped zero stays a scoped observation; discovery coverage remains limited/unknown.

**Proposition-relative path:** actual current domain-owned non-final proposition/candidate → admitted InvestigationNeed with a discriminating target/materiality/basis → scoped request/admission/attempt → observation and result validation → owning evaluation → successor knowledge/lineage. Raw claim status `unresolved` is not enough to create this path. A missing semantic evaluator is an evaluation-capability gap, not automatic justification to search again. Continued discovery needs its own bounded relevance rationale.

**Semantic path:** a source interpretation/candidate proposal can be submitted without first making an executable request. Structural admission does not establish meaning. Evaluation outcomes are `evaluated`, `unsupported`, `failed`, `stale`; an evaluated domain result may itself legitimately be unresolved/refuted/conflicted under that owner's contract. Do not merge these two vocabularies.

**Update validity:** the same target plus an unrelated Workspace revision change does not invalidate a delayed observation. Main checks original request meaning/scope and material bindings before current ingestion. Target change, changed premise, source association or candidate basis requires explicit revalidation/rebinding or historical/stale treatment. New same-target counterevidence can cause successor evaluation; it does not erase an earlier record.

**Stop path:** Investigator proposes stop → main records the proposal; finish/limit/interruption ends the run procedurally → admitted adequacy owner may evaluate the actual remaining justified opportunities. If no method exists, record unsupported adequacy and open obligations. Report/synthesis projections receive that distinction; no justified stop, safety or action follows automatically.

### Known-case walkthroughs for design/regression only

- **HTTPX:** generic discovery may find the upstream removal and target consumer. Investigator proposes an affected TestClient path. Reference Starlette forwarding is not installed target binding. If an admitted evaluator owns the proposition but binding remains unresolved, a real need can justify inspecting exact dependency/constraint evidence. If no such evaluator owns it, record unsupported evaluation; do not create a fake binding assessment. Illustrative CI observations remain illustrative, and missing/expired log endpoints remain scoped. Even a useful source interpretation cannot authorize proceed.
- **Pytest:** a root reference to `doc/en/changelog.rst` is addressable retained-content pressure, not external-only absence. Exact reads remain possible even if the native Markdown-window capability does not support RST. Assigning a 9.0.0 warning to 9.0.3 remains a semantic error even with valid references. Static pytest commands, partial exact-head historical successes and installed-version exercise remain different facts. An Investigator stop proposal while a feasible release read remains does not establish canonical adequacy.

These walkthroughs specify desired distinctions, not model input selectors, gold answers or an H1 success result. HTTPX/pytest remain development/regression cases; repeated improvements there earn no unseen or product acceptance.

## 5. Comparison and causal controls

Both are single adaptive Investigators on the same **main-owned canonical lifecycle**. The treatment is the offered structured access/view-and-fact-capability bundle; it is not a comparison of alternative Workspace architectures. This bundle includes representation and access to existing native mechanical facts, so gains cannot be attributed to serialization alone.

`A0-B3` is deliberately **not described as the unmodified historical Agent**. Contract-2 has no explicit request basis, canonical proposal/evaluation/refinement or adequacy lifecycle. ### Cost accounting details

Charge rejected/malformed requests and corrections; distinguish requested, admitted, dispatched and completed counts. Track underlying source/backend invocations, native producer/evaluator invocations and view operations separately; 48 is not permission to bundle unlimited native work. Any materially larger native capability needs an admitted bounded operation weight/limit agreed before freeze; otherwise it is not offered. Include elapsed native work and model waiting in trial time; record shared preparation separately. Count view/provided evidence bytes and repeated input tokens, generated/reasoning tokens, request fit, truncation, packing, failures and unknown usage. Unknown timeout usage remains unknown/lower-bound rather than zero. Extra metadata consumes the shared envelope; that overhead is part of the treatment cost.

## 6. Proposed qualification and failure taxonomy

No qualification executes here. Later qualification needs a main-owned implementation/seam and explicit Build/run authorization.

**Non-model interface/conformance qualification:** main-owned inputs/fixtures and scripted producer consumers exercise identity and native-authority preservation; objective-before-candidate versus genuine non-final need; rejection without attempt; positive/zero/empty/failed observations; shape-only proposal admission; evaluated/unsupported/failed/stale evaluation; same-target unrelated-revision validity versus material-basis/target change; refinement/counterevidence successor lineage; availability/delivery/citation distinctions; bounded omission/reference resolution; stop proposal/procedural end versus unsupported/current adequacy; one-way report projection and attempted authority escalation. This proves exercised mechanical routing, not semantic competence or recovery implementation. Native facts are compared with the actual native owner results, not model-authored expected states.

**Control bridge fidelity:** exact raw-source/tool observations and scope are preserved; lineage/request basis is explicit; type/reference mapping never strengthens authority; final output remains an attributed Contract-2 report. Shared note/reference and empty-file routes must work or be explicitly unavailable in both arms. Any bridge change affecting the interpretation question is disclosed, not hidden as an unchanged baseline.

**Real-model interface qualification, if subsequently selected:** two short fresh canary sequences per arm/deployment, ≤6 calls each. Exercise later follow-up, a positive read, scoped zero/empty or failed read, correct request basis, proposal/reference delivery and stopping proposal. Main fixture supplies a genuine native non-final premise for the need route; the LLM is not asked to manufacture it. Product-native unsupported outcomes remain explicit. Proposed ceiling: 8 canary sequences/48 calls + 8 development assignments/128 calls = 176 attempts. No runs are selected now. Both arms must qualify before paired case execution for a deployment; failure withholds the pair and retains every attempt/cost. No unqualified diagnostic exception or substitute deployment.

Qualification rejects authority/identity violations even if JSON validates. Canaries do not require semantic success on all proposals; semantic errors are reviewed as errors, not covered by the qualification pass. A host/projection that upgrades a proposal, labels illustrative data as native runtime proof or invents justified stop fails interface qualification and blocks that configuration.

| Failure class | Required separation / recorded result |
|---|---|
| Product integration not available | Missing canonical seam/native prerequisites/capability; no H1 execution. Not a model failure. |
| View/reference/identity defect | Wrong target, original hash basis, delivery disclosure or loss of native meaning; host/adapter defect versus malformed model reference attributed separately. |
| Objective/need/proposal admission | Missing/invalid basis, unsupported refinement, rejected references/scope; no execution or authoritative proposition inferred. |
| Request admission | Unauthorized/unoffered/out-of-budget request rejected; no source absence. |
| Attempt/backend outcome | Inaccessible/failed/malformed/unusable result, actual empty observation, actual scoped zero, interrupted/in-flight outcome remain separate. |
| Evaluation capability/outcome | Unsupported evaluator role versus failed evaluator execution versus stale basis versus evaluated non-final domain result. No automatic acquisition loop. |
| Source interpretation | Wrong subject/operation/polarity/version/timing, availability-as-absence, capture/binding promotion or unsupported entailment. Separate contradicted from unsupported. |
| Applicability/coverage | Omitted consumer/route/candidate, declaration-to-activation promotion, incomplete evidence/path/discovery coverage and global-negative inflation. |
| Stopping/uncertainty | Premature stopping proposal, limit/failure termination, missing adequacy method, hidden feasible discriminator or critical unknown bypassed in advice. |
| Output/recommendation | Empty/malformed final, unsupported recommendation, reference-valid but unsafe scope or attempted execution permission; no output salvage. |
| Resources/method drift | Fit, model/config change, timeout/unknown usage, truncation, packing, budget exhaustion, identity loss or undeclared prompt/tool change. |
| Review failure | Missing source obligation, unmeasured time, inadequate independence/coverage, unresolved reviewer disagreement. Preserve uncertainty; do not claim accepted accuracy. |

## 7. Evaluation and actual reviewer effort

Keep the existing [nine dimensions](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-07-broader-agency-evidence-interface/evaluation-dimensions-v2.json) unchanged, including their recorded hash. Operational collection below elaborates measurement; it does not redefine dimensions or introduce a weighted winner score.

| Existing dimension | H1 measurement responsibility |
|---|---|
| Relevant evidence coverage | Per preregistered obligation: retained/recoverable facts, delivered content/reference, observable examination/use (`unknown` if unobservable), cited and separately supported. Keep evidence/path-model/candidate-discovery coverage distinct. Count undiscovered feasible obligations and unavailable native routes. |
| Interpretation correctness | Review every material proposition in claims, summary, conditions, notes/refinements and report; exact subject/operation/polarity/version/timing/source/capture association. Evaluate native fact use as well as prose. |

| Stopping quality | Assess stop proposal separately from procedural end and any native adequacy outcome. Reviewer checks remaining feasible discriminating actions/coverage limits against the retained universe and actual capability/budget. Missing native adequacy is not marked justified stop or automatic model error. |
| Recommendation usefulness | Supported specific explanation/proposed next work or honestly unresolved advice under unchanged authority. Compatibility/proceed language remains reviewable untrusted output. Product acceptance/action permission is a separate question. |
| Reviewer effort | Actual active source-check/triage/revision/adjudication time, role/assistance and task/result; protocol below. Proxy claim/correction counts reported separately. |
| Resource cost | Every model/native/request/view/preparation attempt, tokens including reasoning, bytes, elapsed, recovery/corrections, packing/truncation, withheld pairs and unknown costs; shared budgets and overhead reported. |

**Later unseen evaluation requirements, without choosing cases:** use source obligations sealed independently of model outputs and hidden from the Investigator/prompt/tool selection. Freeze interface, prompts, native availability, budgets, source-sampling/review rules, seeds and sample/replication size before inference. HTTPX/pytest cannot enter the unseen set. A reviewer not involved in case-specific tuning/answer construction must assess source meaning; neither an AI critic nor the experiment author establishes independent acceptance. If independence is unavailable, label the study assisted/exploratory and preserve that debt. A newly discovered material obligation is retained as a separate supplemental finding with the original denominator preserved, not silently edited into preregistration.

### Active-effort measurement protocol

For each review task, record immutable artifact/subject identity, anonymized reviewer/role, assistance, timer start, pause/resume and end timestamps. Sum non-overlapping active intervals; UTC event timestamps plus displayed local timezone keep clock interpretation explicit. Never infer active time from the elapsed session or model-run duration.

Measure separately:

- source-obligation preparation before output exposure (shared case preparation, not charged twice to both arms);
- first-pass report/source verification and failed-run triage;
- identifying/performing needed semantic revisions to a reviewable useful result, retaining the original immutable model output;
- disagreement resolution/adjudication and integration/maintainer review, where actually performed.

Pause for unrelated work, breaks and inference waiting; record interruptions/clock anomalies and unmeasured intervals rather than inventing zero. Count actual source lookups, corrections, rejected/incomplete artifacts and unresolved disagreements as separate effort descriptors.
