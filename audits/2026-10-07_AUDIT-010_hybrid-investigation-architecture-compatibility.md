## Decision supplied by Ali and review boundary

Ali selected the hybrid evidence-grounded investigation architecture as the intended product direction, independently of the pending interpretation-only experiment:

```text
deterministic evidence acquisition / identity / scope / mechanical semantics
→ structured investigation evidence/workspace
→ adaptive semantic and investigation layer
→ independent evidence/proposition validation
→ maintainer-facing synthesis
→ separately controlled recommendation/execution authority
```

This selects a responsibility direction, not an implemented capability or a particular Investigator. Fixed versus Agent, one versus multiple agents, one versus two interpretation stages, model, framework, exact tools, persistence and broader execution authority remain unselected. The broader research branch is the laboratory for Investigator mechanisms; its completed interpretation-only source adjudication informs the next source-interpreter experiment, not whether this higher-level direction may be adopted.

The full relevant product responsibility remains evidence-backed dependency-update decisions for public Python maintainers. This review classifies existing responsibilities and identifies integration deltas; it does not narrow that product to the two currently implemented impact mechanisms or to source-only API extraction. Source changes, a new ADR, semantic-role promotion, research integration and execution expansion are outside this review. No new controlling owner is created merely to repeat the accepted trust flow.

## Concrete producer → consumer traces and actual gaps

1. **ADR-0010 package-state path:** provider workflow/command facts → dependency-owned operation/precedence facts → CI composition with reused consumption and correlation → per-command `RuntimeDependencyStateResult` → investigation snapshot → report/synthesis. The scoped composer is the sufficient owner of its package-state proposition. A future workspace should reference its result/provenance, not recompute it or turn it into a compatibility claim.
2. **Support-drop path:** authoritative interval → complete structural source window → adopted extractor's untrusted candidates → `validate_support_drop_candidates` → exact target declaration/range comparison → mechanism-specific proposition/applicability evaluation. This is already a small hybrid path. Literal reconstruction does not independently corroborate meaning, and absence of this role's claim does not establish broad impact absence.
3. **API v2 path:** acquired declared-source window → frozen model-bound request → one provider call → shape/reason/span checks → producer-reconstructed citations → separate assisted meaning/coverage review → saved recovery. Mechanical admission is deliberately distinct from semantic acceptance; its typed output cannot be treated as accepted applicability input without additional admission.
4. **Investigation/presentation seam:** the application currently records before/after states for a fixed Python-support investigation, plus other typed evidence. It lacks a general product proposal/evaluation ledger and adaptive cross-candidate state interface. The report preserves some exact text but expressly omits full workflow/source files, model/prompt/config identity and complete operation history. Missing provenance must remain missing; offline report opening cannot reconstruct a resumed investigation.
5. **Proposition provenance seam:** `PropositionAssessment` has state/coverage/owner/detail but no general claim-to-source/delivery/adjudication contract. Existing mechanism owners supply its meaning. Accepting that record directly from a broader Investigator would create an unsupported authority boundary; any new adapter must retain proposed status until the owning evaluation actually establishes the proposition.

Candidate discovery/source examination and investigation of an already formulated non-final proposition are distinct responsibilities. The existing support-drop activation path is justified for that role; it is not a universal ban on separately admitted target/source discovery before a support-drop claim exists. Broader discovery must preserve its own scope/coverage and authority rather than fabricate a candidate merely to activate the old selector. This preserves the Product Decision Model's proposition-relative investigation semantics without freezing the whole product to today's discovery route.

Independent validation here means independent ownership of admission/evaluation and explicit support for the proposition. It does not promise automatic validation of every natural-language claim, and a second model repeating a claim is not independent source corroboration. Mechanical identity/scope/type checks can be deterministic; irreducible meaning still needs an admitted assessment method, counterevidence and honest unresolved states. Delivery proves request inclusion, not attention or understanding.

## Architectural amendment recommendation

**No new ADR or specification amendment is required merely to adopt this intended direction.** The accepted owners already support its evidence/semantic/validation/synthesis separation. Do not restate them in a hybrid ADR.

The genuinely consequential future decision is the **product-owned workspace composition/update and Investigator proposal/admission interface**: which owner assembles recoverable context; how independently acquired records and attributed proposals are represented without losing native meaning; how requests bind to current identity/capability policy; and how admitted observations/evaluations update knowledge. A focused ADR is warranted when that concrete structural method is selected, especially if it fixes a cross-module representation, update or persistence policy. The review identifies that decision; it does not choose its classes, implementation or storage now.

Amend a specification only if the later interface introduces a stable invariant or decision meaning not already covered. Core §§6.1–6.3 already own retention/authority; the Product Decision Model already owns coverage, lineage, evaluation and stopping. Amend ADR-0010 only for a changed execution/package-manager/state-composition method, and ADR-0011 only for changed source-association or permitted examination/admission effects. Neither change follows from hybrid intent alone. Giving models final recommendation authority would separately require reconciling the synthesis specification; it is expressly unselected.
