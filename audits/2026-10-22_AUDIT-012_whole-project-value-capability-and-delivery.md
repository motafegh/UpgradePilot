# AUDIT-012 — Whole-project value, capability and delivery

Recorded October 22, 2026. **Inspected product revision:** `afdd2b4a`; fetched `origin/main` matched it. `2107b98b` subsequently preserves this audit's boundary and verification checkpoint without changing executables. **Authority:** non-controlling audit evidence, for discussion before selecting further work. [Progression record](../working-memory/2026-10-22_whole-project-distance-audit_cycle.md).

## Assessment and scope

UpgradePilot has a coherent evidence-first product direction and substantial verified engineering. Its strongest demonstrated capabilities are exact/scoped evidence handling, explicit unsupported states, bounded native reasoning, report preservation and laboratory controls. Its central unresolved question is whether the normally reachable product removes enough maintainer work to justify its complexity and operating cost.

The main project risk is an imbalance: internal proof, retention and experiment infrastructure can continue improving while useful end-to-end coverage and independent user evidence remain narrow. This is a prioritization judgment, not proof that the architecture is unnecessary, that every abstention is useless, or that a rewrite would help.

Inspected: Charter/route, direction/report/Workspace/Investigator execution owners, accepted source-association and Workspace decisions, relevant normal application/source/report/action paths, hosted verification configuration, and recent retained case/results. This audit is a cross-owner sample, not exhaustive review of every parser, security boundary, plan, experiment or historical artifact. Older audits retain their own original evidence horizons. No model, target code, new public-case acquisition, dependency installation or reviewer outreach occurred.

## Findings ordered by investment importance

### F1 — High: independent product value remains the main unresolved acceptance question

**Observed:** The Charter thesis is incremental useful/calibrated assistance beyond a transparent baseline. The [retained two-case CI comparison](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-20-retained-native-ci-baseline-comparison/COMPARISON_REVIEW.md) found no material addition for HTTPX and limited static/runtime specificity for glyphsLib. It did not establish action improvement or independent usefulness. The report usefulness plan explicitly distinguishes implementation, context presentation and actual maintainer tasks. The two new [HTTPX](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-22-matched-report-source-capture/REVIEW.md) and [OpenCV](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-22-opencv-matched-report-source-capture/REVIEW.md) captures are mechanical input preparation. Reviewer/adjudicator availability and final allocation remain unconfirmed, rather than proven absent.

**Consequence/judgment:** More accurate infrastructure is worthwhile when it unlocks a discriminating value test. It can become indefinite preparation when the human/protocol prerequisite remains unresolved. Honest uncertainty can prevent a false inference, but many explicit unknowns alone do not prove assistance.

**Smallest disposition:** Before another substantial preparation or reasoning build, select the concrete maintainer task and decision the result can change; resolve participant/adjudication feasibility and the task/source-access contract under the existing [report usefulness plan §5](../plans/MAINTAINER_REPORT_PRESERVATION_AND_USEFULNESS_EVALUATION_PLAN.md#5-independent-usefulness-evaluation). Do not create a parallel evaluation program. No reviewer contact is authorized by this audit. If that study is not feasible, record a reasoned deferral and choose a separate bounded product question rather than continuing preparation automatically.

**Status:** Known acceptance debt; current evidence makes it the primary strategic concern, not a newly reproduced code defect.

### F2 — High: normally reachable product coverage is substantially narrower than the mission

**Observed:** `synthesize_maintainer_action` always returns `abstain` and explains its admitted proof boundary. The normal application reaches Python-support interpretation only after `UpstreamRepositoryEvidence` and authoritative interval evidence. The resolver requires project-link/publisher-provenance agreement. Both newest normal cases stop that upstream branch with missing usable provenance; OpenCV separately reaches an artifact candidate, but not target applicability. Runtime correlation intentionally refuses unnamed/matrix/reusable shapes outside its first class. Broad repository-specific API/caller relationships are not supplied by those normal branches.

**Consequence/judgment:** Component maturity cannot be summed into broad decision-support coverage. Serial prerequisites can make the reachable useful subset small even when every local component behaves correctly. We have not measured prevalence, overall abstention rate or useful coverage across public Python updates; these few exposed cases cannot supply those estimates. Conversely, non-abstention is not a prerequisite for a useful report.

**Smallest disposition:** Explicitly choose which end-to-end user benefit the next milestone must deliver: a useful bounded evidence explanation, or a particular richer impact/exposure capability. Account for excluded common input shapes and re-entry conditions. Preserve current strong contracts. [ADR-0011](../docs/architecture/ADR-0011-explicit-source-association-bases-and-proposal-boundary.md) already permits separately attributed declared-source examination; the [existing experimental acquirer](../experiments/api_change_source_acquisition.py) demonstrates that this is not an entirely new architectural idea. Its existence is not normal product adoption. Re-enter that route only on a selected capability/value question and its own proof.

**Status:** Explicit support/coverage debt. No basis here for loosening the resolver, matching job keys as runtime names, treating skipped steps as execution, or granting a positive action.

### F3 — High: semantic reliability remains a demonstrated failure of the tested broader method

**Observed:** The [evidence-first comparison](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-19-investigator-evidence-first-build/COMPARISON_REVIEW.md) judged all four real semantic outputs inadequate, including contradicted signature claims, invented forwarding and overstated CI. The later [native CI presentation review](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-21-investigator-native-ci-presentation/REVIEW.md) confirms the original model had received the same four native records per case. Delivery absence does not explain those failures. Shared deterministic CI presentation preserves owned meaning but does not correct source interpretation in other prose.

**Consequence/judgment:** The tested broader candidate has not earned adoption. Adding structured references, more tokens, a validator role or another model does not by itself repair meaning. The separately accepted [bounded support-drop role](../docs/architecture/ADR-0006-bounded-local-support-drop-semantic-extractor.md) has a different contract/evaluation and is not invalidated merely by these broader failures. No universal model-capability conclusion is justified.

**Smallest disposition:** Keep model proposals separate from facts/actions and retain the negative results. If product value exposes a specific missing relation, compare a simplest credible extraction/interpretation method with alternatives on that responsibility, including explicit false/missing-claim rejection and later independent/protected evidence. Avoid another prompt/model/framework sweep without a decision-changing hypothesis and cost bound. A second model's agreement is not independent corroboration.

**Status:** Known semantic defect in experimental output, still unresolved by retention/presentation work; not a demonstrated current CI-owner defect.

### F4 — Medium: developer guarantees exceed what the ordinary CLI user receives

**Observed:** `investigate_public_pull_request` documents volatile default publication and retained legacy report fields. `project_investigation_report` consumes that result and invokes current synthesis. The CLI exposes report save/open, not Workspace store/recovery/continuation. The saved report explicitly says code revision/model/prompt/config identity and complete operation history are not recorded and cannot replay analysis. The diagnostic captures supply additional code/source receipts outside the normal product. [ADR-0012](../docs/architecture/ADR-0012-canonical-investigation-workspace-and-recovery-boundary.md) and the [migration plan](../plans/INVESTIGATION_WORKSPACE_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_MIGRATION_PLAN.md) require later consumer cutover and retirement; they do not equate native reconstruction with full delivery.

**Consequence/judgment:** The system has valuable tested foundations, but its public operational story remains incomplete. Default users cannot assume they have received the diagnostic's exact reproduction envelope or a recoverable investigation. Two temporarily carried state surfaces impose maintenance/proof cost, although this audit has not demonstrated divergent domain truth or justified deleting either. Full replay remains a separate Charter outcome with its own activation, input and equivalence proof.

**Smallest disposition:** For the chosen product milestone, explicitly retain/defer the remaining migration and delivery obligations, with a concrete consumer trigger. Finish a warranted consumer path or keep the staged scope clear; do not add more generic lifecycle/storage machinery without a current need. Reassess minimal normal run/method identity where reproducible reports are required. Source bodies remain private; local retention is not device-loss protection. Clean install, supported restore/recovery and replay require their respective proofs rather than another codec-only pass.

**Status:** Intentional staged delivery and provenance debt, not an assertion that current save/open violates its declared contract.

### F5 — Medium: three captured cases would not automatically make the proposed utility test informative

**Observed:** Both new captures have zero returned exact-head runs and no admitted runtime-state candidate. OpenCV supplies artifact publication changes with unresolved target applicability; HTTPX supplies upstream/CI limitations. The historical Soup Sieve candidate supplies a support-drop/non-overlap finding with static CI uncorrelated. Its fresh counterpart has not been acquired here. None of those observations establishes a positive command-completion contrast. The [report plan §5](../plans/MAINTAINER_REPORT_PRESERVATION_AND_USEFULNESS_EVALUATION_PLAN.md#5-independent-usefulness-evaluation) requests materially distinct responsibility pressures, source/task/access equivalence, independent labels and at least two eligible reviewers; it forbids building an engine capability merely to fill a quota.

The new source packets contain the normal producer's actual requests/consumed data, not a complete source tree or every source an ordinary maintainer could consult. HTTPX's strong upstream gate means that packet does not contain normally unrequested release/source evidence. How reviewers navigate raw API bodies versus ordinary PR/CI/release pages remains part of the unqualified task setup.

**Consequence/judgment:** A third case may add a valuable positive finding, but a numerical denominator is not responsibility coverage. A packet-only trial can legitimately measure report explanation over fixed inputs; it cannot automatically demonstrate improvement over unrestricted ordinary review or isolate presentation benefit if navigation differs. This is a design risk to resolve before task collection, not proof that the pilot is invalid.

**Smallest disposition:** Freeze the exact question, supported responsibility contrasts and baseline navigation/access before further study spend. Either provide fair task affordances and acknowledge the restricted source horizon, or separately name the broader acquisition-plus-assistance question. Do not inject richer simulation conclusions into normal reports, silently substitute case quotas for coverage, or expand runtime owners to manufacture a study witness. Soup Sieve remains a candidate, not the audit's selected next action.

**Status:** Sharpened current protocol/readiness gap; comparison not yet executed, so no outcome or bias magnitude is claimed.

### F6 — Medium: lifecycle and milestone presentation need focused reconciliation

**Observed:** [The active audit index](active/README.md) still describes AUDIT-009's October 2 next action-relative work and AUDIT-010's source Build/persistence/integration as unselected. Those descriptions are stale relative to subsequent accepted/implemented Workspace and Investigator work, even though their original dated audits remain valid history. The [scheduled index](scheduled/README.md) retains an older planner checkpoint. The [Investigator plan §6](../plans/INVESTIGATOR_INTEGRATION_AND_COMPARATIVE_EVALUATION_PLAN.md#6-explicit-disposition-of-the-older-action-planner-obligation) requires its separate disposition; [October 16 closure](../working-memory/2026-10-16_2301_adapter-d-e-closure-handoff.md) explicitly defers fixture/API repair to that owner before ordinary continuation, and MEMORY retains the debt. Therefore a claim that the obligation was entirely forgotten or that its trigger was certainly violated would be unsupported.

**Consequence/judgment:** Several historical labels/continuations can compete for attention while one live owner remains authoritative. Current MEMORY carries long retained model/accounting history and successive superseded continuations. Detail is valuable evidence, but concentrating it in the live entry point makes the next product obligation harder to recover. Artifact count alone is not proof of excess bureaucracy.

**Smallest disposition:** After discussing this audit, reconcile affected lifecycle labels and one chosen product milestone in existing owners; keep detailed historical outcomes in dated records. Give older planner/migration/utility/semantic obligations explicit retain/defer/reject/re-entry dispositions where needed. Do not mass-rewrite frozen protocols, create another master plan, or trigger an old model evaluation merely because it exists. `MEMORY.md` remains the sole live-position owner.

**Status:** Concrete stale current-facing lifecycle wording plus coordination risk; no product-runtime defect established.

## Evidence supporting retention rather than lower-owner repair

The [fresh/current CI diagnostic](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-20-current-ci-owner-diagnostic/PAIRED_REVIEW.md) is particularly important: all 19 frozen jobs lack `head_sha`, while fresh normal acquisition supplies/validates all 19. The native CI wrapper's newline defect was a real consumer defect and was repaired with a failing-then-passing boundary regression; its original failed output remains preserved. That is an example of fixing an exposed defect without weakening evidence contracts. This audit identifies no additional executable defect requiring an immediate repair.

## Recommended discussion and decision sequence

1. Keep the Charter's evidence-backed hybrid direction provisionally; no broad rewrite or new framework is justified by this review.
2. Choose the next externally assessable maintainer benefit under the existing direction/report owners. State the useful task, current coverage, omissions, cost and reject/inconclusive result. Do not use artifact completion or non-abstention alone as success.
3. If the independent report trial is feasible, settle reviewers/adjudication, source/navigation contract and responsibility coverage, then decide whether the third normal capture is the cheapest missing preparation. If not feasible, explicitly defer that trial and select one concrete product capability gap instead.
4. Reconcile the small set of affected live/lifecycle/milestone owners after that decision. Keep migration, replay, operational hardening, old planner and learning ownership visible without performing them all now.
5. Select one bounded Build only after the decision. No inference, source-owner expansion, reviewer contact, action permission or release follows from this audit.
