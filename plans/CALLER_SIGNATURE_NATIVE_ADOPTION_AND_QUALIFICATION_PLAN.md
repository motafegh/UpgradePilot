# Caller/signature native adoption and qualification design

**Artifact status:** proposed native-adoption design and execution contract for review; implementation and qualification execution require separate activation.  
**Responsibility:** deliver qualified caller/signature source evidence through product acquisition, canonical Workspace capture, reports, source inspection and offline checkpoint recovery.  
**Parent:** [upstream API and target-exposure plan](UPSTREAM_API_CHANGE_AND_TARGET_EXPOSURE_FEASIBILITY_PLAN.md).  
**Design source horizon:** October31 main76757f4e and the linked dated evidence; recheck executable seams before Build.  
**Live selection and execution results:** [MEMORY.md](../MEMORY.md) only.

## 1. Product question and adoption decision

Help a public Python dependency-review maintainer answer:

> Which relevant call was examined, what arguments reach the selected old/proposed dependency definitions, what local binding or keyword-reception result follows, and which source, version, import or dispatch premise remains unresolved?

The proposed disposition is **adopt the qualified source-evidence capability conditionally**, subject to contrasting ordinary-case qualification and the complete native delivery proof below. This is a planning recommendation, not an already implemented product feature or permission to execute the plan.

The full owning responsibility includes indirect calls, supported initializer/protocol paths, honest unknowns and negative evidence, exact source access, failed/partial attempts, historical recovery, understandable presentation and eventual usefulness evaluation. A direct-call-only demo, an experiment file displayed from the CLI, or a codec without a consumer does not complete adoption.

Actual installed versions, execution, behavioral compatibility, interpretation of upstream change meaning, technical-impact applicability and maintainer action retain their separate owners. A source-only adoption must make their absence visible and provide a concrete next evidence question; it must not silently claim to solve them.

## 4. Scope: complete source-evidence delivery, controlled expansion

Include the qualified source-selection and comparison responsibility, not just whichever syntax appears in the additional case:

- Exact native dependency transition and shared initial PR observation/description/changed-file coherence.
- Target declarations and relevant Python usage, exact old/proposed dependency source, explicitly labelled constraint-selected library samples and source-association problems.
- Ordered/scoped bindings, direct and relative/star re-exports, repeated/decorated declarations, initializer/base paths, guarded conditional-import base hypotheses, and the already qualified limited allocation/getter/factory/call-protocol sequence.
- Local positional/keyword/receiver comparison, individual keyword reception, explicit unsupported/dynamic ambiguity, coverage gaps and costs.
- Durable source/material closure and completed, partial, refused, failed, interrupted or unknown-completion histories at their proper owners.
- Product report, saved-report/source inspection, supported checkpoint reconstruction and historical compatibility.

Temporarily defer new syntax families, arbitrary Python execution/dispatch, installed-environment reconstruction, full behavioral compatibility and combined-profile orchestration. These exclusions reflect missing proof methods or independent budgeting responsibilities, not the convenience of a passing case. Record their product cost: findings remain conditional and the source profile cannot answer CI/runtime questions that it did not evaluate.

Re-enter an exclusion when a real maintainer question identifies a decisive missing proposition and an owned method/proof can resolve it. Do not alter a lower owner simply because that proposition is legitimately unsupported. Fix a demonstrated defect at its earliest sufficient owner, preserving prior captures and separately qualifying the changed method.

In particular, source-association timeout/unavailability, conflicting identity and adverse provenance keep distinct meanings. Conditional-import hypotheses do not populate an established lexical/runtime binding. Seeing a variadic keyword receiver establishes local reception only, not semantic acceptance or forwarding by its implementation.

## 7. Native material and representation contract

Proposed family names are `caller_signature_sources` and `caller_signature_comparison`, each with codec version1. They name source evidence, not impact or compatibility. Both support an explicit not-evaluated value. A missing family in a genuinely older revision is historical non-recording, not an evaluated empty result.

| Family | Required material | Actual input references |
|---|---|---|
| `caller_signature_sources` | Acquisition outcome; exact qualified source catalog; role/version/source-association metadata; declarations/sample-selection bases; module questions and refusals; transport policy/events/recovery; selection, omissions, acquisition times and method identity. | `investigation_inputs`; refer to the existing PR-description record where actually consumed rather than duplicating it. |
| `caller_signature_comparison` | Selected analysis source members; file completeness and source facts; candidate/root/call/signature/path/receiver links; ordered witnesses and premises; comparisons and keyword reception; coverage/work/limits; method/source bindings; explicit unavailable/failed outcome when applicable. | Exact `caller_signature_sources` record and `investigation_inputs`; additional actual material only if independently consumed. |

The source family retains successfully acquired relevant text, including material excluded from the bounded analysis selection, subject to its declared acquisition/material limits. Analysis selects its32-file/4MiB universe explicitly from those retained identities. Files that cannot be retained are material omissions; their facts cannot support a positive comparison. Acquisition failure after useful sources must preserve those sources independently of analysis success.

Reuse the existing retained-source catalog representation/validation within an explicit owner-specific payload. Delegate only that representation's checks; do not embed opaque arbitrary experiment JSON as native authority. Source membership, role and content digests must resolve without AST processing. Retain native method/version and actual input records separately from catalog/fact identity and decoder identity. Product role labels should be `target_repository`, `old_dependency`, `proposed_dependency`, `constraint_sample`; transfer tests map the historical experimental labels explicitly.

Use purpose-specific acquisition receipts without broad parallel binding tables. This is a deliberate product retention choice: canonical source material plus required source facts/path/comparison basis and receipt evidence supply the admitted consumer; broad unconsumed transient acquisition analyses do not earn a second truth representation merely because old trials retained them. Original experimental outer snapshots remain unchanged. Any additional observation uniquely needed for a result or failure diagnosis must be retained explicitly before its producer discards it, not reconstructed on open.

Do not copy all source bytes into both new payloads. Comparison records reference retained source/material identities. Do not discard exact spans, decorators, ordered parameters, repeated declarations, trace alternatives or unknowns merely to shrink output. Retention/resource refusal is explicit; nested catalogs/payloads still obey their own and enclosing resource guards. No encoding trick or global-limit increase is selected.

New screening requires its own finite source-inspection budget/activation; the exhausted earlier screening/attempt cannot be silently extended.

This sharpens selection for one contrasting arm, not the full product boundary: framework-mediated and limited protocol paths retain their existing qualification pressures.
