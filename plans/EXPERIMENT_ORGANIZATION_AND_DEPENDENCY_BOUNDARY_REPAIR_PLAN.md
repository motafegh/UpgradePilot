## Shared signature-evidence support

Extract one cohesive module, `experiments/api_signature_evidence.py`, for AnalysisLimits, fixed retained source/fact/comparison decoding, terminal/source rendering and bounded IO used by BOTH curated v1 and ordinary v2. Use responsibility-bearing public helper names. Keep selection preparation, ordinary discovery/acquisition, specific worker admission/attempt protocols and CLI ownership in their existing runners. Do not create a generic common/framework layer or merge the two different attempt protocols.

The existing runner paths and old public imports remain available as compatibility surfaces. No new active runner imports another signature runner's private names. The shared owner may use its own private implementation helpers; privacy is module-local, not a filesystem move requirement.

Alternatives: documentation alone leaves the real private/runner coupling; promoting helpers in-place removes underscores but leaves two runners coupled through curated orchestration/method identity; mass folders or generic common utilities widen hash/navigation cost without an independent responsibility. One evidence-support extraction addresses the actual identical semantics and two consumers, while preserving paths elsewhere.
