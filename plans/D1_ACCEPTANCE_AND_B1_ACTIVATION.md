## Accepted conclusions

Ali accepts, at the current bounded planning depth, that:

1. S001–S005 provide sufficient contrasting evidence to define the first credible
   implementation responsibility without requiring S006 merely to continue.
2. UpgradePilot needs a complete evidence-to-decision runtime rather than a report-first
   vertical slice.
3. The recurring logical responsibilities include invocation, exact frozen identity,
   operations, evidence states and provenance, observations/interpretations/findings,
   transparent baseline, conditional activation, bounded decision or abstention,
   synchronized reports, transitions, review/ownership state, and validation.
4. Advisory analysis, adapter compatibility, repeated execution comparison, failure
   attribution, dynamic reproduction, platform analysis, dedicated stopping/cost state,
   and separate dependency/PR decisions remain conditional.
5. CI color has no sufficient authority without tying the proposed dependency identity
   to the exact revision, environment, command, responsibility, and result.
6. The transparent baseline is a comparator: S004 showed it may be sufficient; S005
   showed it may select the wrong broad action.
7. Investigation should stop when no remaining question can materially change the
   action, uncertainty, required checks, activation state, or product conclusion.
8. The first executable core should begin from real-shaped replay fixtures so runtime
   state and invariants can be proven before live acquisition is added.
9. Simulation artifacts are discovery evidence, not approved production schemas.
10. UpgradePilot remains decision support and does not claim target safety, automatic
    semantic reliability, automatic merge authority, production readiness, or
    independently demonstrated Ali capability.

## Accepted limitations and deferred questions

Acceptance preserves rather than removes these limitations:

- the five cases are contrasting evidence, not a representative corpus;
- no final unresolved-comparison case has been completed;
- separate dependency and PR decision dimensions remain conditional;
- production persistence, replay storage, acquisition, services, deployment, models,
  agents, graphs, and advanced infrastructure remain unselected;
- semantic automation reliability remains unproven;
- S004/S005 clean-checkout validation was degraded by the local DNS failure;
- Ali-owned implementation capability must be demonstrated during later building work.

These items do not currently block B1. A new case or method may be admitted later only
when a named implementation or evaluation uncertainty requires it.
