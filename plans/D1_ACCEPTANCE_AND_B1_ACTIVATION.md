## Acceptance statement

After reviewing the S001–S005 findings, the evidence-to-decision runtime model, universal and
conditional responsibilities, stopping and baseline results, and claim limits, Ali accepted
the D1 synthesis and authorized the project to leave D1 and enter B1.

## Accepted conclusions at the transition

Ali accepted, at that bounded planning depth, that:

1. S001–S005 provided sufficient contrasting evidence to define the first credible
   implementation responsibility without requiring S006 merely to continue.
2. UpgradePilot required a complete evidence-to-decision runtime rather than a report-first
   vertical slice.
3. Recurring logical responsibilities included invocation, exact frozen identity, operations,
   evidence states and provenance, observations, interpretations, findings, a transparent
   baseline, conditional activation, bounded decision or abstention, synchronized reports,
   transitions, review or ownership state, and validation.
4. Advisory analysis, adapter compatibility, repeated-execution comparison, failure
   attribution, dynamic reproduction, platform analysis, dedicated stopping or cost state,
   and separate dependency and PR decisions remained conditional.
5. CI colour lacked sufficient authority without tying the proposed dependency identity to
   the exact revision, environment, command, responsibility, and result.
6. The transparent baseline was a comparator: S004 showed it could be sufficient, while S005
   showed it could select the wrong broad action.
7. Investigation should stop when no remaining question can materially change the action,
   uncertainty, required checks, activation state, or product conclusion.
8. Simulation artifacts were discovery evidence, not approved product schemas.
9. UpgradePilot remained decision support and did not claim target safety, automatic semantic
   reliability, automatic merge authority, production readiness, or independently
   demonstrated Ali capability.

The transition initially expected a replay-first executable core. During B1, Ali rejected
that sequence because prepared fixtures and internal lifecycle terminology obscured the real
public PR workflow. The later accepted B1 gate superseded that sequencing conclusion while
preserving replay as supporting test and reproducibility behavior.

## Accepted limitations

The transition preserved these limitations:

- five cases were contrasting evidence, not a representative corpus;
- separate dependency and PR decision dimensions remained conditional;
- production persistence, replay storage, acquisition, services, deployment, models, agents,
  graphs, and advanced infrastructure were unselected;
- semantic automation reliability was unproven;
- S004 and S005 clean-checkout validation had been degraded by a local DNS failure;
- Ali-owned implementation capability remained to be demonstrated through building work.

These statements describe the evidence position on 2026-07-23. Later evidence is owned by
its dated records and live continuation by `MEMORY.md`.

## B1 authorization granted by this transition

The record authorized B1 to:

1. inspect source, tests, package configuration, commands, and outputs;
2. classify inherited implementation as retained, corrected, superseded, experimental
   evidence only, or separately justified removal;
3. freeze the minimum credible executable responsibility;
4. define the boundary between prepared evidence and behavior the product must execute;
5. choose the smallest reversible representation and interface;
6. define B2 acceptance tests and Ali-controlled implementation work;
7. create one bounded B2 plan after the responsibility was accepted.

It did not authorize, at that time:

- B2 product implementation before the B1 gate passed;
- continuation of the superseded M2-S03 plan;
- target-repository mutation;
- premature selection of a database, service, queue, model, agent, graph, or deployment architecture;
- a production-ready or capability claim.

The later B1 gate record preserves the resulting requirements and exit evidence:
[`B1_IMPLEMENTATION_RESPONSIBILITY_FREEZE_REQUIREMENTS.md`](B1_IMPLEMENTATION_RESPONSIBILITY_FREEZE_REQUIREMENTS.md).

This historical transition record has no live continuation authority.
