## Original responsibility

The old plan attempted to compose supplied or replayed case/evidence data into a
deterministic decision plus machine-readable and human-readable reports.

That responsibility was useful but incomplete.

## Why it was superseded

S001–S003 showed that a credible first executable core also needs an
evidence-derived treatment of:

- runtime invocation and discovered/frozen identity;
- material operations and acquisition failures;
- raw/reference preservation;
- evidence states and authority;
- claims, interpretations, findings, and supersession;
- transparent baseline comparison;
- complete decision and transition behavior;
- follow-up, rerun, and new-run boundaries;
- factual review, Ali review, external confirmation, and capability state;
- conditional responsibilities such as failure attribution.

The old route also assumed repository context, acquisition, persistence, replay,
and evaluation could be sequenced mechanically after a report-first slice. That
assumption is no longer controlling.

## Retained evidence

The following remain useful candidates and existing source/test evidence where
actually implemented:

- strict case and evidence contracts;
- raw evidence and attributed-claim boundaries;
- explicit missing and rejected states;
- deterministic decision authority;
- application, machine-report, and human-rendering separation;
- provenance-backed report statements;
- no-model supported operation;
- changed, missing, invalid, and security-boundary tests;
- negative model experiments and rejected phrase/category controls.

They are not automatically accepted as the next implementation scope. B1 of the
controlling route must inspect and re-admit them against the complete evidence.

## Premature or rejected assumptions

Do not carry forward without new evidence:

- supplied/replayed evidence as the complete normal input boundary;
- report composition as the entire first runtime responsibility;
- caller-supplied semantic answers disguised as automated work;
- only two current decision outcomes as the final supported policy;
- repository context and acquisition as automatically later concerns;
- resuming implementation under the old M2 label.

## Future use

At B1, compare this plan with:

- current source and tests;
- S001–S005 evidence;
- the runtime artifact and baseline specifications;
- the accepted minimum credible runtime responsibility.

Re-admit individual requirements only when they remain necessary and correctly
scoped. Do not reactivate this plan wholesale.
