## Full responsibility, bounded contribution and exclusions

The [Charter](../PROJECT_CHARTER.md) requires repository-specific evidence-backed decision support, including useful interpretation, sources, uncertainty and justified next work. This increment contributes faithful presentation of **already-owned CI meaning**. It does not establish that a model interprets source relationships correctly or that the resulting assistance is useful to maintainers.

The first proposed delivery is a deterministic CI-only view over the four admitted native families, with sources, scoped positives, unresolved propositions and explicit retention limits. The original laboratory interpretation remains separately attributed and inspectable, with its actual evaluation outcome or explicit unavailability. Model summary/recommendation/claims are not copied into the trusted CI view, used to overwrite it, or presented as its accepted overall conclusion. A pointer/label alone is not semantic validation of that proposal.

This is a deliberate narrowing: a combined model-authored semantic report remains unqualified. Excluding its prose from the owned CI conclusion is justified because the existing host has no admitted semantic proposal evaluator and the instruction-only comparison failed. Interpretation is retained, not silently repaired or discarded. Re-enter combined semantic presentation when a separately selected method can support the relevant propositions and every visible conclusion, including summaries and recommendations. No wrapper can count the historical failed reports as adequate.

No new acquisition, correlation class, dependency-state witness, semantic checker, critic/model arm, model prompt/schema change, target execution, public CLI redesign, saved-report schema migration, action permission, full consumer migration or replay is selected. Missing-name/matrix support, HTTPX caller/callee interpretation, historical glyphsLib step provenance and independent usefulness remain separate responsibilities.

## Design choice and alternatives

**Recommend reuse through a narrow CI-only projection seam, then one experiment-owned presentation consumer.** Do not copy the CI interpretation rules or build a competing truth model.

| Option | Fit and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Continue current laboratory publication unchanged | Retains useful proposal evidence, but does not supply the selected deterministic final CI view |
| Attach a native appendix while endorsing the model's overall summary/recommendation | Insufficient: contradictory free text still appears as the accepted conclusion |
| Call the full product report projector | Reuse it when an actual complete application result exists. The four-family experiment capture does not supply that result; do not fabricate unretained fields to invoke it or synthesize an action |
| Expose shared CI projection and keep proposal authority separate | Recommended: reuse existing meanings, preserve useful positives and unknowns, avoid another inference/validation owner; limited to the owned CI view |
| Add another prompt, keyword contradiction filter, worksheet or semantic critic | Not selected. Instructions already failed; source-specific filters violate generality, and a genuine semantic evaluator is a separate method/proof responsibility |

The proposed internal Python entry point consumes admitted CINativeProjection values (or explicit existing typed fields from that projection), and returns a small CI presentation record composed of existing ReportAssessment/ReportFact/ReportFinding/ReportUnknown/ReportSource records, exact identity and limits. It contains no newly inferred state or action. Both it and the full report projector use **one** CI mapping implementation. Collector arrangement and the smallest record/container name are routine Build choices; the dependency/authority/output boundary above is the design decision. Direct calls into today's private accumulator are not a supported CI interface.

The experiment consumer obtains native material through the existing admitted boundary. It records exact target, checkpoint/revision/material identities and available producer/version/retention gaps alongside the projected view. An optional interpretation reference binds original bytes and its actual assignment/proposal/target context through trusted retained metadata, never the model's self-description. Missing interpretation is explicit; wrong/ambiguous target association refuses the join. Reuse an existing bound publication record when available; do not create a new general provenance subsystem.

No proposal text participates in the deterministic CI summary, findings, unknowns or conclusion. No supposedly safe model subset is selected by keywords. The proposal remains an independently inspectable laboratory artifact, with unsupported/not-evaluated meaning preserved. A future combined report must resolve its own semantic acceptance; side-by-side display cannot discharge it.

## Ordered implementation and proof boundary

1. **Qualify the supported seam and exact inputs.** Review the bounded Build scope, native input identities and expected proof obligations. Use the exact historical HTTPX/glyphsLib selections in [the retained freeze](../working-memory/evidence/2026-10-20-retained-native-ci-baseline-comparison/freeze.json), revalidated on owned copies. Preserve original checkpoint/result bytes and the historical observation horizon. The old comparison verifier requires all96 product files to match its historical source freeze: it cannot be reused unchanged after legitimate report extraction. Do not weaken it. A new composition freeze must separately validate original input membership/hashes, record the exact intentional report-only source delta, and verify unchanged native codec/reconstruction/CI semantic owners before claiming compatibility. Retain producer-version gaps; unchanged representation/source does not retroactively supply missing semantic-version metadata. Final executable/test identities are frozen after implementation/proof and before new case outputs. Do not rerun the old baseline/model comparisons or invent a complete application result.
2. **Share the existing CI projection and rendering.** Expected product edit anchors are report_projection.py, report.py and responsibility-owned tests. Preserve normal full-report and saved-report meanings; no public report-file format change. The CI-only route must not invoke maintainer synthesis, network, models, source parsing or domain evaluators. It is a presentation consumer, not a new producer.
3. **Add one narrow experiment consumer.** Expected home: an experiment-owned CI presentation helper and its tests, using the shared product seam and existing native reconstruction/access. Emit an explicitly versioned experiment artifact and human view. Keep the original model proposal/reference separate; do not alter REPORT_SCHEMA, publication admission, existing run results or model/controller behavior. This is an explicit offline/new consumer artifact, not a silent change to Raw/Structured treatment or a resumed old trial.
4. **Prove semantic parity and boundaries before real-case composition.** Compare the CI-only output with the CI portion of a genuine normal application report under equivalent admitted inputs. Only source-ID allocation may be normalized through a bijection preserving content and the reference graph. Preserve state/reason/detail/proposition/strength, positive findings, missing-versus-unperformed states, command-completion scope, unknowns and source identities. Test empty/no-run, static-not-correlated, supported runtime, mixed supported/unresolved commands and not-evaluated outcomes using actual producers where practical. Controlled positive fixtures are labelled, not claimed as real current CI coverage.
5. **Freeze and exercise original retained inputs once as a new offline composition proof.** Publish the exact executable/tests/input identities, output policy, caps and review rules before producing case outputs. Two requested native case rows: HTTPX no exact-head runs at the historical capture; glyphsLib supported static intent with uncorrelated execution/unresolved manager environment. Record both availability/refusal and completion denominators, preserve failures and unavailable proposals, and verify originals unchanged. Compare the new CI view to the retained native values, not a hand-authored answer. This does not change historical adequacy or demonstrate general usefulness.
6. **Review every presented surface and stop.** Confirm original model summary/recommendation/claims/conditions/stopping text cannot enter the owned CI conclusion, including a structurally valid but deliberately contradictory synthetic proposal. This proves output selection/authority separation, not automatic semantic detection. A new projection failure remains operational/input unavailability, not semantic abstention. Preserve any original successful laboratory publication separately if subsequent composition fails. Deliver evidence-backed learning and a separate next-investment decision.

FCI001–FCI003 remain immutable boundary pressure: normal acquisition can supply heads; identity does not establish skipped execution; missing names remain unsupported. Their diagnostic outputs are not complete four-family native CI captures or dependency-state witnesses. Do not fabricate that missing context to count them as three end-to-end composition cases. Re-enter full FCI composition only after separately admitting the needed native preparation/capture; the existing lower-owner results remain valid evidence at their own seam.

Focused regression anchors are tests/test_report.py, tests/test_report_file.py, the actual native reconstruction tests and experiment composition/publication tests. Broaden only for a changed seam. Recheck existing report/save/CLI compatibility where extraction affects it. No fresh model inference is needed to prove deterministic projection or adversarial output selection.

## Budgets, pass condition and stop line

For a separately selected Build: at most three active engineering hours including preparation, implementation, validation, review, records and publication. One two-case offline composition proof, at most30s/case,60s aggregate and120s startup-inclusive whole execution; all new public/private evidence at most5MiB. Use a monotonic external watchdog/receipt and retain partial failures. Do not introduce a new generic controller to enforce these small caps. Zero model predictions/loading, GPU, new acquisition, target execution, dependency installation or extra cases. A cap failure preserves an incomplete result and stops; no automatic retry/refreeze. Synthetic regression tests are machinery proof, not additional real-case evaluation arms. Previously unmeasured capture/model/human costs remain unknown, not zero.

Pass means a truthful CI-only consumer exists, shares the admitted mapping, preserves positive and unresolved meanings/source closure, handles input/composition failures honestly, keeps proposal text outside its owned conclusions, and leaves original frozen input/checkpoint/result bytes unchanged. All requested rows remain in the denominator, including explicit unavailable outcomes; an unavailable row cannot count as a completed presentation or evidence of case coverage. Inadequate proof is reported as partial/inconclusive, not accepted by reducing the obligation after execution.

Independent maintainer usefulness, better overall reports, corrected model interpretation, more accurate actions and full-product acceptance are **not** pass claims. At handoff revisit the [existing usefulness plan](MAINTAINER_REPORT_PRESERVATION_AND_USEFULNESS_EVALUATION_PLAN.md#5-independent-usefulness-evaluation): this design does not establish equivalent ordinary-review packets or independent reviewer readiness and authorizes no reviewer contact. Lack of a new model result is not a universal reason to postpone a ready utility study.

Planning completion does not activate this Build. Source/test mutation and the offline composition proof require the concrete bounded design to be selected; inference, combined semantic presentation, new support policy or material architecture changes require their own decision. If an admitted-contract defect is demonstrated during implementation, preserve its original evidence and repair it at its responsible owner within scope; legitimate unsupported material does not justify widening a lower owner.
