## Exact candidate task goals

### PR context goal

```text
Using only the supplied packet, produce the existing full report. Use claims first to state material source observations and their exact scope, then the relationships those observations support and the premises that remain unresolved. Cite the supplied passages needed for each material relationship. Trace the declared dependency change through visible consumer use and any supplied provider changes. Where CI material is present, connect workflow steps with the supplied tool configuration and dependency inputs before drawing coverage conclusions. Distinguish static declarations, historical observations, exact-context correlation and established runtime facts; preserve native assessments at their stated strength. Derive summary, conditions and recommendation from those findings, with a specific justified check or scoped conclusion for material missing bindings. Do not infer compatibility, low risk, executed coverage, source exhaustion or action permission from a version category, a successful job, an empty tool menu or absence of a discovered issue. Preserve directly observable findings as well as uncertainty. Return only the existing report object, ordinarily at most 600 words without hiding material uncertainty.
```

### Captured consumer goal

```text
Using only the captured consumer and supplied before/after callee sources, produce the existing full report. In claims, identify the captured source identities and scope, compare accepted callee parameters across the supplied signatures, and identify the arguments actually forwarded by the captured call. Relate the material parameter changes to that actual call: distinguish a supported source-level effect, a change that does not affect this call, and a relationship that remains unresolved. Cite the passages needed for each side of a material relation. Do not invent forwarded arguments or substitute general library knowledge for the supplied call. These reference sources are not the target's observed installation or execution; a scoped finding does not establish whole-update compatibility. Use existing status and missing_observation fields to distinguish source observations, inferences and missing bindings. Base summary, conditions and recommendation on those findings and state justified next work where needed. Return only the existing report object, ordinarily at most 600 words without hiding material uncertainty.
```

These request visible factual work products, not an internal-thought trace. Claim ordering is an instruction target; different ordering alone is not semantic failure. Correctness, material coverage, support and scope control acceptance. Model-authored claims cannot become new evidence or corroborate one another.

## Adequacy and recommendation safeguards

Use the unchanged20 frozen obligations and adjudication discipline. A case is adequate only when every material obligation is supported or specifically justified unresolved, usable source scope is preserved, there is no decision-critical false/unsupported advice or authority promotion, and a scoped conclusion or useful justified next work follows. Review every report field, including conditions, stopping reason and recommendation, not only claims or citation count.

Make the known unsafe route explicit without adding a new structural gate:

- Compatibility, low-risk, verified-coverage or action reassurance fails when its material premises are unsupported, contradicted, hidden or stronger than their evidence. Empty recommendation attribution cannot excuse that advice; a nonempty list cannot establish entailment either.
- `asserted`, `inferred` or `unresolved` labels do not override actual content. A cautious sentence elsewhere cannot repair a material false premise or reassuring conclusion.
- Scoped abstention or a targeted inquiry may be justified by an explicit missing binding, with its basis and source relationships made traceable. An empty recommendation-evidence list alone is not an automatic semantic failure for every kind of recommendation. Do not demand an invented citation for an uncitable absence/problem state.
- Even a source-correct experimental recommendation carries no product action permission. Core authority and synthesis requirements still apply before accepted use.

These clarify the original zero-unsupported-advice gate; they do not retroactively regrade the reference or add an unrelated status/list-length test. Preserve original labels and source reasons before explicit adjudication. Consequential unresolved reviewer disagreement makes the decision inconclusive. Reviewer-only protocol/prose, original reports, adjudication answers and this plan must not enter model construction; verify an allowlisted construction path rather than banning words legitimately present in source.

The reviewer is AI-assisted and source-aware with prior exposure, not blinded independent human acceptance. Compare source facts, relations, uncertainty, final advice, case-level adequacy and actual cost. Record genuine partial improvements without turning a failed case into a pass. One historical sample versus one new sample per exposed case cannot establish causality, reliability, statistical improvement or model ranking, even after reducing the intentional change to instructions.
