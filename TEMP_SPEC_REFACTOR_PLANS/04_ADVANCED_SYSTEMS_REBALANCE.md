## 3. Preserve the exposure-level model

Retain the distinction:

```text
A0 orientation
≠ A1 hands-on exposure
≠ A2 project-integrated pilot
≠ A3 adopted product responsibility
≠ A4 independent advanced capability
```

Retain strict claim language and the rule that exposure does not equal mastery.

## 4. Replace the default breadth target

### Required A0 orientation for all six

- Kubernetes;
- microservices;
- distributed queues;
- multi-cloud systems;
- bounded autonomous multi-agent systems;
- advanced MLOps.

A0 must include:

- the problem the technology addresses;
- main responsibilities and components;
- simpler baseline;
- major trade-offs and failure modes;
- relationship to the UpgradePilot mission;
- what evidence would justify deeper exposure.

### Required A1 hands-on selection

Select two or three areas according to:

- demonstrated project relevance;
- prerequisite readiness;
- expected information gain;
- career-learning value;
- available capacity;
- current ownership health;
- feasibility, safety, cost, privacy and cleanup constraints.

An A1 package must remain executable, project-connected where technically credible, bounded, observable and cleaned up.

### Required A2 project-integrated pilot

Target at least one A2 when:

- a representative project workload exists;
- a simpler baseline exists;
- comparison criteria are defined;
- the pilot does not endanger the core;
- Ali can obtain meaningful ownership evidence.

### Conditional second A2

Authorize a second A2 only when:

- the core end-to-end product remains on track;
- evidence and evaluation quality are healthy;
- Ali’s ownership is not falling behind system complexity;
- the pilot is not artificial;
- enough capacity remains after core obligations;
- the first pilot did not reveal a larger prerequisite deficit.

### Stretch outcomes

- A1 for remaining areas;
- a second A2 when conditions later become true;
- any A3 permanent adoption.

These are evidence-dependent and must not be used to declare the route failed merely because they were not reached.

## 5. Negative-decision evidence

For every area not admitted to A1 or A2, record a concise decision:

```text
Technology/area:
Current project need:
Prerequisite state:
Simpler baseline:
Reason not admitted:
Opportunity cost:
Safety/cost/privacy constraints:
Reconsideration trigger:
```

An evidence-backed reject or defer decision is valid technical judgment, not a failed technology requirement.

## 6. Capacity rule

Replace a fixed 20–30% obligation with a bounded, reviewable allocation.

Advanced exposure may receive substantial capacity only after:

- the first credible core flow exists;
- core behavior is testable and reproducible;
- current milestone obligations are healthy;
- Ali has sufficient ownership of the representative workload;
- the selected package has a defined question, baseline, ceiling and cleanup plan.

At formal reviews, capacity may be increased, reduced or deferred based on product progress and ownership evidence.

Only one major advanced exposure package should normally be active at a time.

## 7. Admission rule for every package

Before authorizing A1 or A2, answer:

1. What exact engineering or career-learning question is being tested?
2. How is it connected to UpgradePilot?
3. What is the simpler baseline?
4. What evidence will distinguish value from novelty?
5. What prerequisites must be present?
6. What is the time and scope ceiling?
7. What new failure modes, costs and maintenance appear?
8. What Ali-owned reasoning, operation, test or diagnosis is required?
9. What causes rejection, cleanup or reconsideration?

## 10. Validation scenarios

### No credible multi-cloud need

Record A0 orientation and a defer decision with reconsideration triggers. Do not create a two-provider exercise solely for checklist completion.

### A real asynchronous limitation appears

A queue may enter bounded decision/exploration mode. A1 or A2 still requires a baseline, scope ceiling and ownership plan.

### Core ownership falls behind

Pause or reduce advanced exposure. Protect the core and Ali’s ability to explain, modify, test and diagnose the representative workload.

### One pilot proves no value

Reject and clean up the pilot. The result remains valid engineering evidence.
